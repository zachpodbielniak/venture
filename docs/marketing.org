#+title: Marketing audiences, permission and delivery

* Approval is a frozen audience

The =marketing= module requires =mail=, =sequences= and =leads=. Its records
use the ordinary forms, API, CLI, actions, related-record sections and audit:
=marketing_list=, =marketing_member=, =marketing_consent=, =marketing_send=,
=marketing_recipient= and =marketing_event=. The service is a policy boundary;
mail transport remains the replaceable =VentureMailer= interface and the
organization-owned SMTP adapter.

Create a list, record explicit marketing permission, and create a draft send
with its subject, text and optional HTML. The *Preview and freeze audience*
action resolves that list once and retains every eligible recipient and
exclusion, together with the rendered content. Review the counts and related
recipient records before *Approve frozen send*. There is no implicit approval
at preview or enqueue. A preview cannot be rerendered or edited: create another
draft to incorporate changed filters, members, addresses or copy. Approval
retains its actor and time. Neither an import nor an ordinary record write can
manufacture consent, approval, delivery identities or tracking evidence.

* Static lists and saved searches

A static list has =marketing_member= rows, each naming exactly one contact,
company or lead from the list's organization. Duplicate member identities are
refused. A company means its own primary =email=, not every employee. A contact
or lead means that record's =email=. Mixing those three kinds in one static
list is supported. No implicit company-to-contact expansion occurs.

A segment has =mode=segment=, a target record type, and URL-encoded saved
search filters, for example =status=new&source=conference=. Fields and filter
operators are the ordinary typed query implementation. Organization, history,
ordering and pagination overrides are refused; results are always scoped to
the list's organization and ordered by record ID. A preview refreshes the
segment; an approved send never refreshes it. Narrow an audience larger than
10,000 source records instead of accepting a silently truncated preview.

Addresses are trimmed and ASCII case-folded, matching the existing sequence
suppression rule. They must be single mailboxes, without display names,
recipient lists or header characters. Provider-specific aliases such as dots
or plus suffixes are not guessed to be equivalent. The first eligible source
in stable membership/record order wins a duplicate address. Missing sources,
invalid addresses, absent permission, suppression and duplicate addresses
remain separate preview decisions. An earlier source without permission does
not prevent another explicitly permitted source at the same address from
being selected. Suppression applies to every source at that address.

Templates reuse =VentureMailTemplate=: ={name}= and other readable fields of
the selected source are available, underscores and dashes use the ordinary
wire convention, and HTML substitutions are escaped. Sensitive fields are
never template values. Unknown placeholders fail the whole preview
transaction. Mixed record kinds should use fields all selected kinds share.
Each body is limited to 64 KiB before and after rendering; the subject is
single-line and at most 998 bytes. A tracked body may contain at most 1,000
web destinations.

* Permission is evidence, not a contact flag

The contact, company and lead actions *Record marketing permission* require
an actual nonfuture timestamp, a source, an evidence statement/reference and
a replay identity. The retained evidence binds the source record, its current
normalized address and the =marketing= purpose. Repeating the same identity
and evidence returns the same record; reusing the identity for other evidence
is refused. The =withdraw:= identity prefix is reserved for internal withdrawal
evidence. This is an operator's record of permission, not a claim that
Venture independently verified a signature or a legal basis.

Ordinary contact creation, import, merge, conversion and address edits do not
create permission. Marketing subject references declare =RETAIN_REFERENCE=:
CRM merge preserves the original consent, approved recipient and static-list
member source while ordinary CRM references follow the survivor. A queued
recipient whose source was merged away is cancelled; add the survivor to a
list and record its permission explicitly. A new address or a new CRM identity needs its own explicit
permission. A queued recipient still binds the original evidence and address;
it is cancelled if that source is removed or its address changes. Historical
consent remains retained, including after the original CRM subject is removed.
Withdrawal is another evidence record linked to the permission it withdraws,
not an edit erasing what was previously recorded.

Unsubscribe, hard bounce and complaint use the existing organization-scoped
=suppression= record and sequence exit mechanism. Suppression is retained
through ordinary imports, merges and address changes. New permission does
not clear it. The initial preference policy is deliberately one organization-
wide marketing stop, without automatic resubscription or topic-specific
preferences. A necessary invoice, receipt, password notice or other
transactional message is outside this marketing policy.

* Execute, pause and recover

*Execute bounded delivery* examines at most 100 eligible recipients, queues
at most one due recipient, and attempts that exact outbox message through the
configured organization's mail account. The public service's =run()= operation
only enqueues; applications may deliver through =VentureMailOutbox= themselves.
A missing organization SMTP binding is a visible outbox failure, never a
fallback to installation-wide credentials.

The interval is 1–86,400 seconds, with a 60-second default. Both enqueue and
actual submission retain their next permitted time. There is at most one
unresolved outbox identity per send. A repeat sweep, process restart or
transport retry uses the same recipient key, outbox key and Message-ID.
The final read-only =before-transport= hook rechecks the exact address, consent,
suppression, approval and tracking permission after mutable hooks and provider
preparation have completed. A withdrawal during either step cancels submission
without spending the retry budget. The outbox's synchronous =before-claim=
hook defers paused/throttled work without changing identity or spending retry
budget. Module disable also defers pending marketing mail.

*Pause* keeps the approved snapshot and queued work. *Resume* continues it.
*Cancel* stops future recipients and cancels only known queued/failed mail;
it cannot retract accepted messages or resolve an in-flight/uncertain attempt.
The outbox's existing backoff handles known temporary failures. An uncertain
SMTP outcome blocks further recipients for that send until an operator reviews
and explicitly retries the retained outbox message. A retry may send a second
copy if the relay accepted the first: this is not exactly-once SMTP delivery.
Never turn uncertainty into an automatic fresh recipient or Message-ID.

Scheduling is explicit: invoke the bounded action or call the service from an
operator-controlled schedule. There is no marketing database worker thread.
The recipient's =state= is campaign queue bookkeeping. Once =mail_id= is set,
read that linked outbox message or =marketing_performance= for the authoritative
SMTP outcome; an enqueued recipient does not itself prove delivery.
The generic outbox can also be delivered independently; the same pause,
throttle and permission hooks still apply.

* Public preferences and one-click unsubscribe

Preview requires =server.base_url= to be a public HTTPS origin; loopback HTTP
is accepted only for development. The origin is frozen into the send. Each
eligible recipient receives a separate 256-bit random unsubscribe capability.
The lookup uses its SHA-256 hash; raw delivery links live only in sensitive
recipient/outbox content and are omitted from generated record responses and
audit diffs.

=GET /marketing/u/<token>= displays a generic preference confirmation without
changing anything or disclosing a contact/address. Syntax-valid unknown tokens
receive that same page. =POST= to the same URL with form data
=List-Unsubscribe=One-Click= performs idempotent withdrawal. The capability
selects its retained organization/address; no login, cookie, caller-supplied
organization or redirect is needed. A malformed/unknown capability changes
nothing. POST bodies are bounded to 1 KiB. The confirmation and response have
no-store and no-referrer headers.

Withdrawal cancels pending marketing recipients and applicable sequence
outreach, including previously queued mail. It does not claim to retract an
already submitted message. The capability remains valid after the contact is
deleted and after the marketing, mail or sequences module is disabled, so old mail can still
honor its preference link. Replays preserve the original withdrawal time.

HTTPS deliveries carry =List-Unsubscribe= and =List-Unsubscribe-Post= headers
following [[https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8058][RFC 8058]]. The configured SMTP relay must DKIM-sign both headers before
mail leaves the sender's domain; Venture does not own the relay's signing
keys. Loopback development links appear in the body but do not advertise
HTTPS one-click headers. Verify the relay's actual signed output before
claiming mailbox-provider one-click support.

* Bounce and complaint evidence

Use a recipient's *Record delivery feedback* action with =hard_bounce=,
=temporary_bounce= or =complaint=, an actual time, a source/explanation and an
organization-scoped event identity. The recipient must have an actual outbox
attempt and the evidence cannot predate approval. Replays retain one event;
conflicting reuse of an identity is refused. Record the reviewed DSN/provider
reference in the source. This is an implemented operator workflow and a typed
service seam for a future authenticated provider consumer; no unauthenticated
provider bounce webhook is installed.

A hard bounce or complaint creates retained address suppression and stops
pending outreach. A temporary bounce records evidence without suppression.
An SMTP connection, credential or temporary relay error does not prove the
recipient address is invalid. A permanent transport failure also is not
automatically labelled a hard bounce. Only the specifically evidenced
recipient acquires a hard-bounce observation; other messages at the same
address are suppressed, not falsely counted as independently bounced.

* Observations and report meaning

Tracking is off unless both the send's =tracking= and the organization's
=marketing_tracking= are enabled at preview. This is independent of the
sequence switch and of anonymous website analytics. The existing sequence
random-token, link wrapping and transparent-pixel helpers are reused with
explicit marketing routes and retained marketing destinations. Plain-text
URLs remain unwrapped; HTML links and the pixel carry the observations.
The unsubscribe link is appended after wrapping and is never tracked.

=GET /marketing/t/o/<token>.gif= records an open; =GET
/marketing/t/c/<token>/<position>= records a click and redirects only to the
frozen web destination. Unknown tokens or positions record nothing. First
open/click times are retained per recipient, with at most one event per UTC
day, kind and destination. No IP address or user agent is collected. Mail
scanners, image proxies and bots can produce these requests: an observation
is not proof a human read a message. Turning off organization tracking stops
new observations and prevents queued tracked snapshots from being delivered;
create a new untracked send rather than silently changing approved content.

=marketing_performance= is an approval cohort: its period selects
=approved_at=. Each send reports its approved eligible count, preview
exclusions, SMTP-accepted count, pending/uncertain/failed/cancelled outcomes,
hard-bounce evidence and uniquely observed opens/clicks/unsubscribes. Outcomes
are retained through report execution, not a reconstruction of what was known
at the period end. SMTP acceptance is not inbox delivery. Accepted mail can
later bounce; those columns overlap. Observations on an uncertain submission
do not silently resolve that outbox outcome. Reports remain source-traceable
through send, recipient, mail, consent and event references.

* Storage and verification

Field metadata owns all new schema. Paired migration =000420_marketing.sql=
checks organization-scoped membership, consent, recipient and event replay
indexes without inventing permission for existing CRM rows. Disabled modules
may have no tables; enabling marketing later creates the same empty metadata
schema. Approved sends, recipients, consent and events cannot be deleted,
restored or purged through generic writes. Withdrawal evidence always retains the original consent UUID. Its optional
typed reference is populated while the marketing module is enabled; disabling
a module must not prevent an existing recipient from withdrawing permission.
Editable draft lists/members do not
rewrite existing snapshots.

=tests/test-marketing.c= and =tests/test-marketing-mail.c= exercise service
and outbox contracts on SQLite and schema-isolated PostgreSQL. The suite also
runs the real HTTP routes and built CLI, while =test-mail= checks SMTP wire
headers. See [[file:marketing-demonstration.org][the built-artifact demonstration]] and
[[file:testing.org][test coverage and reproduction]].

* Extension boundaries

=VentureMarketingService= owns consent, frozen sends and retained evidence.
Ordinary records remain field declarations, including typed subject references,
enums, indexes and sensitivity flags. New transports implement =VentureMailer=
and its existing preparation/binding hooks; they do not replace the outbox or
marketing eligibility decision. The synchronous =VentureMailOutbox::before-claim=
signal can defer known queued work without consuming an attempt. Mutable
=before-send= hooks run before provider preparation; =before-transport= is the
final read-only veto boundary after account binding is persisted. Final handlers
must not write records, perform transport I/O or iterate a main context. All
hooks run synchronously on the main thread.

CRM dedupe honors =VENTURE_COLUMN_FLAG_RETAIN_REFERENCE= from the same field
metadata (including declarative =retain_reference: true=), so a future evidence
record can preserve its original subject without another hardcoded type list.
The flag does not grant write permission or replace immutable-save validators.
Exports retain the original subject IDs; private capabilities remain excluded.
