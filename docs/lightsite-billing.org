#+TITLE: Lightsite billing

* Two organizations, two Stripe connections

the operator bills each Lightsite customer from *the operator's own* Venture
organization, the /billing organization/: its plans and monthly prices,
its invoices, its dunning and its Stripe connection (=/organizations/<id>/settings/stripe=
on that organization, see [[file:stripe.org][Stripe payments]]). There is no second billing
system; everything below is [[file:billing.org][SaaS billing]] driven through
=VentureBillingService=.

Each customer *business* is an organization of its own, made by
=POST /api/v1/lightsite/signups= ([[file:lightsite-accounts.org][Lightsite account validation]]). It keeps its
own books and connects *its own* Stripe through its own
=/organizations/<id>/settings/stripe=. In the billing organization the
business appears as an ordinary customer company:

| Company field | Value |
|---------------+-------|
| =name= | the business organization's name, when the company is first made |
| =external_id= | =lightsite:organization:<organization_id>= -- the stable reference back to the business |
| =source= | =lightsite= |
| =kind= | =customer= |

Nothing is written to the business's own organization. No key, secret or
account ID of either Stripe connection is ever returned: Lightsite only
learns whether the business has connected its own.

Plans for now, all monthly, managed AI included (no usage lines):

| Code | Name | Price |
|------+------+-------|
| =team= | Plan A | $10/month |
| =growth= | Plan B | $20/month |
| =starter= | Plan C | $30/month (example) |

* The setting

| YAML | Environment | Default |
|------+-------------+---------|
| =lightsite.billing_organization_id= | =VENTURE_LIGHTSITE_BILLING_ORGANIZATION_ID= | =0= |

The ID of the billing organization. =0= (unset) turns every Lightsite billing
route off: an authorized caller gets 404. A configured ID that is not a live
organization is also 404. Nothing ever falls back to the default
organization -- billing from whichever organization happens to be first is
the failure this setting exists to rule out. The value is read on every
request, like the other settings, and needs the billing module on.
Deferred enrollment, the first charge, prepaid terms, made-back measurement,
the one-time guarantee and cancellation are [[file:hosted-billing.org][Hosted enrollment and guarantee]].

* Authority

Exactly the [[file:lightsite-accounts.org][trusted-service sign-up]]'s, through the same check
(=venture_tenant_service_check_trusted_service()=), for every route below
and in [[file:hosted-billing.org][Hosted enrollment and guarantee]], reads included:

- Only =Authorization: Bearer <token>=. No credential, an invalid or expired
  token, or a browser session cookie: 401.
- The token's user must be an active workspace administrator (tenant role
  =admin=) *now and* as captured in the token's =workspace_role= snapshot,
  and the token must be write-capable (not minted with the viewer role):
  otherwise 403. Platform roles are not tenant authority.
- The hosted Host/Origin boundary and lifecycle gate apply: wrong Host, a
  read-only, suspended or changed workspace: 403.

Authentication is judged before the setting, so an unauthenticated caller
learns nothing about whether billing is configured. Every answer, refusals
included, carries =Cache-Control: no-store=.

* =POST /api/v1/lightsite/billing/subscriptions=

Binds a business to a plan.

#+begin_src json
{"organization_id":42,"plan_code":"team","idempotency_key":"bind:42:2026-10-04"}
#+end_src

A closed JSON object of exactly these three members, at most 4096 bytes
(413 above that). A missing, extra or mistyped member, a non-object body,
malformed JSON or an escaped NUL is 422.

| Member | Rule |
|--------+------|
| =organization_id= | JSON integer, positive; a live organization other than the billing organization |
| =plan_code= | =team=, =growth= or =starter=, exactly |
| =idempotency_key= | 1-128 of =[A-Za-z0-9._:-]= |

** What happens

In the billing organization:

1. A receipt for =idempotency_key= is looked up. If its request hash
   (SHA-256 of the three members, length-prefixed in a fixed order) matches,
   the *answer first given* is returned again with 200 and nothing is
   written. A different request under the same key is 409. GET the view
   for the current state.
2. The plan with that code and its one active monthly price are found; a
   plan that is not offered, or has no active monthly price, is 404, and
   more than one active monthly price is 409 (retire all but one).
3. The customer company for the business is found by its external ID, or
   made.
4. With no live subscription (none, or only cancelled or expired ones) the
   monthly price is *started* through =VentureBillingService= with one
   seat: per the [[file:billing.org][billing rules]] a price with no trial issues its first
   month's invoice immediately, through settlement. With a live one
   (trialing, active or past due) it is *changed at the end of the period*:
   today's price stands until renewal, nothing is invoiced now, and the
   change notice is the service's own. Asking for the price the
   subscription is already on, or already switching to, changes nothing.
   A paused subscription cannot change terms (422, the service's refusal).
5. The receipt (key, hash, business, plan code, outcome, company,
   subscription, the answer, administrator) is stored.

A first invoice posts money, so whole-operation accounting approval is
taken before the transaction, as a quote's subscription start does. Steps
3-5 are one transaction: a failure leaves no company, subscription,
invoice or receipt, and the same request can simply be sent again.

A first success is *201* (a start, a change or nothing to change), a replay
*200*; both answer the billing view below.

| Result | Meaning |
|--------+---------|
| 201 | Instruction given now |
| 200 | Identical replay of an earlier instruction |
| 401 | No bearer token, an invalid/expired token, or a session instead of a token |
| 403 | Not a workspace administrator now and in the token, viewer token, wrong Host, workspace lifecycle |
| 404 | Billing organization not configured or unavailable; no such business; plan or monthly price not offered |
| 409 | Key reused for another request; ambiguous monthly price |
| 413 | Body larger than 4096 bytes |
| 422 | Body is not the documented closed object, a member breaks its rule, the business is the billing organization, or the billing service refuses the change |
| 500 | Storage failure; nothing was written |

* =GET /api/v1/lightsite/billing/<organization_id>=

The business's billing view. =<organization_id>= must be a positive 64-bit
integer (422 otherwise) naming a live organization (404 otherwise) other
than the billing organization (422).

#+begin_src json
{"organization_id":42,
 "plan":{"code":"team","name":"Plan A","amount":"10.00","currency":"USD","interval":"month"},
 "state":"active",
 "current_period_end":"2026-11-04T23:34:34.537657Z",
 "owed":{"amount":"10.00","currency":"USD"},
 "overdue":false,
 "failed_payment":false,
 "last_invoice":{"number":"BILL-b991e07f-f3fe-4523-90cd-0156f438212d-2026-10-04","status":"sent",
                 "total":"10.00","due_date":"2026-10-04T23:34:34.537657Z","paid_at":null},
 "stripe_connected":false}
#+end_src

| Member | Meaning |
|--------+---------|
| =organization_id= | The business |
| =plan= | The plan and price the subscription is on *now* (a change at renewal shows after renewal); =null= with no subscription |
| =state= | The subscription's status: =trialing=, =active=, =past_due=, =paused=, =cancelled=, =expired=; =none= with no subscription |
| =current_period_end= | When the period being served ends (it renews then); =null= with no subscription |
| =owed= | The customer's balance in the billing organization -- receivables less unused credit -- in the plan's currency (the billing organization's book currency with no subscription). Negative means credit on account |
| =overdue= | An issued invoice is open and at least one whole day past due -- the receivables aging's rule. Subscription invoices are due on receipt |
| =failed_payment= | A failed payment is on record: the subscription is =past_due= or carries an overdue-since date (=mark-payment-failed=, by an operator or the verified Stripe adapter, until =recover= or =resume=) |
| =last_invoice= | The customer's latest invoice: its number, status (=draft=, =sent=, =partially_paid=, =paid=, =void=), issued total, due and paid times; =null= with none |
| =stripe_connected= | Whether the *business's own organization* has exactly one active Stripe connection. Never a key or an account ID; the operator's own connection on the billing organization does not count |

Amounts are exact decimal strings in the currency's own exponent, never
floating point; times are ISO 8601 UTC, the canonical transmitted form.
A business with no customer company yet is answered, not refused: no
plan, =state= =none=, nothing owed.

Nothing here suspends, pauses or cancels anything. An overdue invoice and a
failed payment are reported so a person can act; dunning steps configured
in the billing organization run as they always do.

* =GET /api/v1/lightsite/billing=

The staff overview: the same view for every Lightsite business with a
subscription (any state) in the billing organization, ordered by
=organization_id=. Companies of the billing organization that are not
Lightsite businesses are not listed.

#+begin_src json
{"customers":[{"organization_id":3,"plan":{"code":"starter",...},...},
              {"organization_id":4,"plan":{"code":"growth",...},...}]}
#+end_src

Above 2,000 customers the request is refused (422) rather than answering a
list that silently stops.

* Staging recipe

Run against the local disposable instance with an administrator's =venturectl=
credentials. =$BILLING= is the operator's own organization ID; use the IDs each
=create= returns for the plans.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl list organization
BILLING=1   # the operator's organization, from the list above

# Settlement accounts, if the organization does not invoice already
# (1000 cash, 1100 receivables, 4000 income; see docs/receivables.org).
venturectl create account organization_id=$BILLING code=1000 name=Cash kind=asset active=true
venturectl create account organization_id=$BILLING code=1100 name="Accounts receivable" kind=asset active=true
venturectl create account organization_id=$BILLING code=4000 name=Sales kind=income active=true

# The three plans and their monthly prices. Managed AI is included.
venturectl create plan organization_id=$BILLING name="Plan A" code=team description="Lightsite Plan A, managed AI included" active=true
venturectl create plan organization_id=$BILLING name="Plan B" code=growth description="Lightsite Plan B, managed AI included" active=true
venturectl create plan organization_id=$BILLING name="Plan C" code=starter description="Lightsite Plan C, managed AI included" active=true
venturectl create plan_price organization_id=$BILLING plan_id=PLAN_A_ID currency=USD interval=month amount='10 USD' per_seat=false trial_days=0 active=true
venturectl create plan_price organization_id=$BILLING plan_id=PLAN_B_ID currency=USD interval=month amount='20 USD' per_seat=false trial_days=0 active=true
venturectl create plan_price organization_id=$BILLING plan_id=PLAN_C_ID currency=USD interval=month amount='30 USD' per_seat=false trial_days=0 active=true

# Check: one active monthly price per plan.
venturectl list plan_price active=true
#+end_src

Then set =lightsite.billing_organization_id: <BILLING>= in the
configuration (or =VENTURE_LIGHTSITE_BILLING_ORGANIZATION_ID=<BILLING>= in
the environment) and restart. Connect the operator's Stripe on the billing
organization through its settings page as for any organization. A price is
immutable once a customer is on it: to change what a plan costs, add a new
price and retire the old one, then move its customers ([[file:billing.org][SaaS billing]],
"In the web interface").

* Verification

=tests/test-lightsite-billing.c= runs a real hosted server with real bearer
tokens, makes each business through the sign-up route and creates the three
plans in the billing organization:

- a new business on Plan A: its customer company (name, external ID, source),
  one active subscription, its first $10.00 invoice issued and owed, and
  the read answering exactly what the write answered;
- an identical replay (200, same answer, no rows) and a reused key with
  another plan or business (409, no rows);
- a plan change at renewal: no new invoice, the switch pending, the plan
  shown unchanged until renewal; asking again changes nothing; the
  down-sell is a change like any other;
- an invoice ten days past due reads =overdue=, and a payment marked failed
  through the billing service reads =failed_payment= and =past_due=, with
  the subscription untouched;
- =stripe_connected= false, still false after the operator connects Stripe on the
  billing organization, true once the business's own organization has its
  binding, with no key or account ID in the answer;
- the staff list: Lightsite businesses only, ordered, each entry the
  business's own view; a business with no subscription answered as =none=;
- every authority refusal on all three routes, including a session cookie
  and a token without the captured workspace role, with no rows written;
- unconfigured and unknown billing organizations (404), body shape and
  bounds, an unknown business, the billing organization itself, a retired
  price;
- rollback after the receipt write fails (no company, subscription or
  invoice left) followed by a successful retry, and receipts refused to
  every generic writer and to deletion.

The suite was written before the implementation and failed in its first
fixture, on the missing setting. The receipt-guard case was confirmed to fail
with the guard disabled. =tests/test-auth.c= includes the three routes in its
anonymous checks.

* Notification facts and financial confirmations

=GET /api/v1/lightsite/billing/<organization_id>/notifications= uses the
existing trusted-service boundary. The response binds =workspace_id= and
=organization_id= and lists minimal events with stable =id= and =kind= values.
It does not return payment methods, provider customer identities or sealed
settings. The hosted caller owns email presentation and persists each handoff
before sending through the mail outbox.

Events cover verified saved methods, a first charge within two days, recorded
receipts, unresolved failed collection, a second installment within two days,
committed cancellation, refunds, credit notes and a committed guarantee result.
Opening balances are not new payment receipts. Cancelled or completed payment
paths do not produce upcoming-installment notices. A later method revocation
does not erase the fact that it was verified. A guarantee email requires an
actual assessment receipt; this read never invents eligibility or grants money.

Customer quote acceptance enqueues its confirmation inside the acceptance
transaction, using the quoted contact or customer email and a stable record
identity, with text and HTML. Customer payments reuse the existing committed
payment receipt and its PDF; no second confirmation is added. Missing addresses
do not reject the financial action. Bookkeeping payments are omitted. Hosted
billing customers are omitted from generic receipts because the hosted
application renders their owner notifications.

On upgrade, any existing generic payment receipt remains owned by its original
outbox identity. The notification feed does not add a second branded receipt,
including when the old message is retrying, uncertain or retained as deleted.

Failed checkout collections and retained failed prepaid attempts both produce
failure facts. A settled installment stops reporting its earlier failure.

Quote delivery reuses the existing private-link outbox implementation from the
quote-mail change. No payment-provider request is made by any notification read
or email renderer. SMTP delivery, retries and uncertain outcomes remain owned
by the durable outbox.
