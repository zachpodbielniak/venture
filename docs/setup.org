#+description: Guided books setup, control-account mappings and first-posting checks

* Setup

The =setup= module requires =ledger= and =periods=. It registers
=accounting_setup= and =accounting_control_map=. =VentureSetupService= owns
preview and complete. Generic writes cannot mark setup complete.

A resumable checklist covers the legal entity, book currency, fiscal year,
basis, tax profile, chart template, banks and opening balances. Preview stores
the answers on =accounting_setup=. Complete applies them: it updates the
organization, seeds a conventional chart when the entity has none, maps
control accounts, generates the fiscal calendar, creates an operating bank
and posts opening cash against retained earnings. Amounts are =VentureMoney=.

Control maps name a semantic classification (=cash=, =receivables=,
=payables=, =tax=, =retained_earnings=, =clearing=, =inventory=, =income=,
=expense=) and an account in the same legal entity. The account class must
match the role: cash is an asset, tax payable a liability, and so on. Bank
and item overrides use =subject-type= / =subject-id=. Dated restatements use
=effective-from=; changing an existing map restates the current default.

Receivables, payables and statements resolve those classifications instead of
spelling chart codes. Renumbering a mapped cash account keeps cash-flow cash
and cash postings on the same account. The first invoice, bill or payment is
refused until the required mappings exist, in bookkeeping language.

The Setup nav item opens =/setup=, which redirects to the legal entity's
=accounting_setup= record. Preview and complete are actions on that page.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl action accounting_setup complete 1
#+end_src
