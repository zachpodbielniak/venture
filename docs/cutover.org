#+title: Accounting cutover
#+description: Guided Zoho Books and QuickBooks opening-balance migration

* Cutover

The =cutover= module requires =ledger=. =VentureCutoverService= owns preview, import, reconcile, activate
and rollback. Generic writes do not create opening journals.

A mapped JSON payload names the source (=zoho_books= or =quickbooks=), an
explicit cutoff, chart mapping, customers, vendors, items, open AR, and bank
balances. Amounts must include an ISO currency (or a payload-level
=currency=). Mapped tax amounts are frozen on the opening invoice rather than
rounded through an integer percent. =open_ap=, =credits= and =assets= are
imported through the payables, receivables and assets modules (below); a
payload naming one of them while its module is switched off is refused, and
anything the mapping cannot express is listed under =unsupported=. Each
source ID is stored on =accounting_cutover_row= so reimport is idempotent.

Open AR is imported as issued invoices (tax split at issuance) rather than as a
second opening journal, so operational documents and opening cash are not
double-posted. Bank opening cash is one balanced journal against equity.
Activation is refused until reconcile has accepted trial balance, issued
opening AR, bank opening cash, open AP, unapplied credits and asset net book
value. Rollback is allowed only before activation: it reverses every opening
journal with a reversing journal and voids the imported invoices and bills.
Generic writes cannot stamp a reconciled state. The Cutover nav item opens the batch
record; preview, import, reconcile, activate and rollback are actions on that
page.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl action accounting_cutover import 1
#+end_src

Rollback is idempotent: repeating a completed rollback leaves its reversal journals intact and cannot recreate opening cash. Opening line unit prices inherit the row or payload currency when their source amount has no ISO suffix. Reconciliation checks each posted journal separately, without netting currencies or overflowing a running 64-bit total across history.

* Open vendor bills

Each =open_ap= row becomes an approved =vendor_bill= through
=VenturePayablesService=, keeping its original =number=, =date=, =due_date=,
vendor (matched by =vendor_source_id= against earlier rows, or created as a
supplier from =vendors=) and =lines=. A line carries =description=, =amount=
(net), a frozen =tax= and an optional =account_code= or =category=; approval
posts the lines against Accounts payable on the bill date, so the frozen tax
is never recomputed. =paid= names the amount settled before the cutoff. It is
recorded as one =bill_payment= dated at the cutoff, method =opening=, applied
to the full bill, so the bill's balance is =lines - paid= rather than a
smaller bill. Because that cash already sits inside the imported bank
balance, the payment's cash leg is offset by an opening journal against
equity 3000. An optional =balance= must equal =lines - paid=; a paid amount
at or above the total is refused (=open-ap-balance=), because such a bill is
not open.

#+begin_src json
{"open_ap": [{"source_id": "bill-1", "vendor_source_id": "vend-1",
  "number": "B-1", "date": "2025-12-10", "due_date": "2026-01-09",
  "paid": "30 USD", "balance": "75 USD",
  "lines": [{"description": "Hosting", "amount": "100 USD", "tax": "5 USD"}]}]}
#+end_src

* Credits

Each =credits= row is a =credit_note= created through the receivables or
payables service: =kind= must be =customer= with =customer_source_id=, or
=vendor= with =vendor_source_id=; omitting =kind= still infers vendor when
=vendor_source_id= is present. It carries =date=, =amount= and an optional
=number= stored as the reference, and remains unapplied so later settlements
can use it. A credit larger than its counterpart's open balance after the AR
and AP imports is refused (=credit-within-open-balance=). Customer credits
need receivables; vendor credits need payables.

#+begin_src json
{"credits": [{"source_id": "cr-1", "kind": "customer", "customer_source_id": "cust-1",
  "number": "CN-1", "date": "2025-12-20", "amount": "20 USD"}]}
#+end_src

* Fixed assets

Each =assets= row is placed through =venture_asset_service_import_opening()=
with =cost=, optional =salvage=, =in_service_at=, optional =acquired_at=,
=method= (=straight_line=, =declining_balance= or =none=),
=useful_life_months= and =accumulated_depreciation= to the cutoff. The three
account codes =asset_account_code=, =accumulated_depreciation_account_code=
and =depreciation_expense_account_code= must exist in the chart; the module
seeds none. History is not recomputed: one opening journal dated at the
cutoff debits the asset account for cost and credits accumulated
depreciation and equity 3000 for net book value, and one posted
=depreciation_entry= for the cutoff month carries the accumulated amount so
the register, disposals and reports see it. The month holding the last
instant before the cutoff, and every earlier month, is history; the schedule
resumes with the month after it, and the remaining depreciable amount
(cost less salvage less accumulated) is spread over the months of life that
remain, so the first post-cutover run continues from the cutoff. An asset
past its life keeps its residual value until disposed. In-service dates
after the cutoff and accumulated amounts outside zero to cost less salvage
are refused (=opening-accumulated-within-basis=). No tax schedule is created.

#+begin_src json
{"assets": [{"source_id": "as-1", "tag": "LAPTOP-1", "name": "Laptop",
  "cost": "3600 USD", "in_service_at": "2025-01-01", "method": "straight_line",
  "useful_life_months": 36, "accumulated_depreciation": "1250 USD",
  "asset_account_code": "1500", "accumulated_depreciation_account_code": "1590",
  "depreciation_expense_account_code": "6850"}]}
#+end_src

* Reconciling and rolling back the three

Reconcile compares the payload's open AP (=lines - paid= per bill) with the
approved bills' balances, the credit amounts with the unapplied =remaining=
balances, and =cost - accumulated_depreciation= with the register's net book
value at the cutoff, in addition to the per-journal trial balance, AR and
bank checks. Any drift, such as a payment recorded after import, fails
reconcile and so blocks activation.

Rollback keeps every imported row and marks it =rolled_back=. Opening
journals, including the asset openings and payment offsets, are reversed by
reversing journals dated at the cutoff. An opening payment is refunded to
its allocation and the bill is voided through the payables service; credit
postings are reversed and remaining is refreshed to zero so the credit cannot
be allocated onto a later bill or invoice; the asset's remaining schedule is
skipped and the asset is written off at the cutoff without a second journal.
AP, credits and asset net book value net to zero and nothing posted is
deleted. Everything runs inside the single cutover transaction: a failure
after the first bill is written leaves no bill, payment, credit, asset or
journal behind. Reimport of the same source ids is a no-op only while those
rows are not rolled back; unique bill numbers and asset tags still occupy the
voided and written-off records.
