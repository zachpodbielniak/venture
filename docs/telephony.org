#+title: Phone calls and text messages
#+description: Click-to-call through your phone system, every call logged on the contact, and two-way texts with consent and quiet hours

The =telephony= module connects Venture to a phone provider. With a 3CX
phone system you call a contact from their page and every call -- made,
taken or missed -- is logged on the contact by itself. With a VoIP.ms
number you text contacts and read their replies on the contact's page. The
module requires =crm=, =activities=, =integrations= and =marketing=: calls
are ordinary call-log activities and text consent is ordinary marketing
consent evidence, so there is no second call log and no second consent
store.

* Setting it up

Open the organization's page and choose *Telephony settings* (owners and
administrators only), or go to =/organizations/ID/settings/telephony=.

1. *Connect a line.* Pick 3CX or VoIP.ms and fill in its fields:
   - *3CX*: your phone system's address (for example
     =https://pbx.example.com=), and the client ID and API key of a 3CX
     API integration ("Integrations, API" in the 3CX admin console) that
     may use call control.
   - *VoIP.ms*: your API user name (your VoIP.ms sign-in email), the API
     password you set under "SOAP and REST/JSON API", and the number texts
     are sent from. Allow this server's address in the same VoIP.ms page.
   Credentials go into the organization's encrypted vault ([[file:integrations.org][Organization
   integration credentials]]) and are never shown again. To rotate them,
   connect the same provider again with the complete settings.
2. *Copy the webhook address.* When a line is made Venture shows its
   webhook address once: =https://your-venture/hooks/telephony/TOKEN=.
   - For 3CX, set it as the CRM integration's call journal address, so
     3CX reports each call when it ends.
   - For VoIP.ms, add
     =?to={TO}&from={FROM}&message={MESSAGE}&id={ID}&media={MEDIA}= and set
     it as the number's *SMS URL callback*. VoIP.ms is answered =ok=.
   *New webhook address* makes another and stops the old one at once.
3. *Add extensions.* Under *Extensions*, give each person who calls their
   extension (for example 101). Click-to-call rings that extension first.
4. *Set the rules*: the phone country, text quiet hours and recording
   links (below).

* Calling

A contact, a deal or a ticket shows a *Call* button with the contact's
number. Pressing it rings your own extension; when you pick up, the phone
system dials the contact. A deal or ticket calls its contact.

#+begin_src text
POST /api/v1/telephony/call   {"contact_id": 12}            (or deal_id, ticket_id, and an optional "number")
-> 202 {"call_id": "4242", "state": "ringing your extension"}
#+end_src

It refuses in plain words, before anything is dialled, when no phone
system is connected, when you have no extension in this organization, or
when the contact has no number Venture can read. It needs the editor role.

* Every call is logged

When 3CX reports a call, Venture writes it through the existing *Log a call*
service ([[file:activities.org][Planned activities and call history]], "Logging calls"):
one completed call activity and one history entry on the contact, with the
direction, the seconds talked, the result and the person whose extension
took it. The provider's call id is the replay identity
(=call_external_source= =3cx-v1=, =call_external_id= the line and the call
id), so the same report delivered twice is one call.

- The caller is matched to a contact by their number: both sides are read
  into international (E.164) form first, so =(555) 010-0123=,
  =555.010.0123= and =+1 555 010 0123= are the same person. Numbers written
  without a country code are read as the organization's phone country (US
  when empty). When several contacts share a number the oldest is used.
- A caller nobody knows becomes a contact named "Unknown caller" plus the
  number, so the call has somewhere to live. An outbound call to a number no
  contact has is not logged.
- A missed inbound call is a call-log row with the result *missed* and
  *For the inbox* set, ready for the shared inbox. An outbound call nobody
  answered is *no answer*.

* Recording links -- off by default

When *Keep call recording links* is on, the phone system's recording link
is stored on the logged call (only a stable =https= address with no query
or fragment; anything else is dropped). It is off by default.

*The recording note, in plain words.* Recording a phone call is allowed in
some places only when everyone on the call agrees, and some places require
you to say at the start that the call is recorded. Venture never records a
call, never downloads a recording and never fetches the link; it only keeps
the link your phone system made. Turn the switch on only if your phone
system announces recording and you are allowed to keep recordings. The
link is kept with the call for as long as the call is (see "Timelines,
reporting and retention" in [[file:activities.org][the call history]]).

* Texting

A contact's page shows their texts, newest last, each with its state:
=queued=, =held= (quiet hours, with the time it will go), =sent=,
=delivered= (where the provider reports it), =failed= (with the
provider's reason) or =received=. Below is the composer.

#+begin_src text
POST /api/v1/contacts/12/texts   {"body": "Your estimate is ready", "hosted_image_id": 0}
GET  /api/v1/contacts/12/texts
POST /api/v1/telephony/run?organization_id=1     send the texts that are due
#+end_src

- A text is at most 1600 characters. With VoIP.ms, a text longer than 160
  characters or with a picture goes as MMS.
- A picture you send is one of the organization's hosted images (the
  designed-email image store); the provider is given its public address.
- A picture you receive is fetched only from a host the line lists under
  *Picture hosts* and kept as a private document (kind =text-media=),
  readable only by people who can read the organization's documents. It is
  never published.
- An inbound text is marked *For the inbox* until somebody texts back.
- Nothing is retried. A failed text stays failed with its reason; sending
  again is a person's choice, because a retry after an uncertain answer
  could text someone twice.

* Consent

Venture texts a contact only when their latest text consent is an
agreement. Consent is kept exactly like email consent: each agreement and
each withdrawal is a retained =marketing_consent= row (purpose =text=, the
number in E.164), with where it came from and the evidence, and the latest
one decides.

- An operator records an agreement on the contact's page (*Record their
  agreement*, with how they agreed), or =POST
  /api/v1/contacts/12/text-consent {"granted": true, "evidence": "..."}=.
  *They asked to stop* records a withdrawal.
- A text that is only =STOP=, =STOPALL=, =UNSUBSCRIBE=, =CANCEL=, =END=
  or =QUIT= (any capitals, spaces and punctuation ignored) withdraws
  consent and is answered once: "you are unsubscribed ... Reply START to
  resubscribe". A second STOP while already out changes nothing and is not
  answered.
- =START= or =UNSTOP= agrees again and is answered once.
- =HELP= or =INFO= is answered with how to stop, at most once a day.
- These answers are the only texts sent without consent and outside the
  quiet hours: they are the answer the person just asked for.
- A send without consent is refused, in words, and nothing is queued.
  The send path checks again just before a provider is asked, so a STOP
  that arrives after a text was queued still stops it.

* Quiet hours

No text goes out between *21:00 and 08:00 in the recipient's own time
zone*: the contact's time zone, else the organization's, else UTC. A text
written during quiet hours is held and sent when they end. An organization
can set other hours (for example =20:00-09:00=) or =off=. An unreadable
setting means the default, never "none". This is enforced in the one send
path every text goes through -- the page, the API, sequences, journeys and
campaigns only queue -- so nothing can bypass it.

* Text steps and text campaigns

- *Sequences and journeys*: a step with the channel *text* sends a real
  text through the channel hook ([[file:journeys.org][Journeys]]). The step queues; the send
  path sends, with consent and quiet hours. A contact without text consent
  is *skipped* with the reason and the journey carries on. Each step texts
  once, however often the sweep runs. The older *text task* channel still
  only reminds the owner.
- *Campaigns*: a marketing send with channel *text* uses the ordinary
  campaign machinery ([[file:marketing.org][Marketing]]): the preview freezes who gets it and
  leaves out anybody without a number (=no_phone=), without text consent
  (=no_text_permission=) or without a connected line (=no_text_line=);
  approval, pausing and the per-recipient interval are the same; each
  recipient is texted once. Every campaign text ends with "Reply STOP to
  opt out." unless it already says STOP. The email warm-up does not apply
  to texts.

* The webhook and how it is checked

=GET= or =POST /hooks/telephony/TOKEN= needs no session. It is checked, in
order:

1. *The address token*: 64 hexadecimal characters from secure randomness,
   stored only as its SHA-256. An unknown or disconnected token is 404.
2. *Allowed senders*: when the line lists addresses (or address/prefix
   ranges) the request must come from one of them, or it is 401. This is
   the connecting address; behind a proxy, list the proxy's view or leave
   it empty.
3. *The provider's signature*, for a provider that signs (HMAC-SHA256,
   compared in constant time).
4. *Idempotency*: a text by its line and the provider's message id, a call
   by log_call's external identity, a delivery receipt by the text's state.
   The same request twice changes nothing the second time and is counted
   as a duplicate.

Honestly: *neither 3CX's call journal nor VoIP.ms's SMS callback is
signed*. For both, the long unguessable address token (kept secret like a
password) and, if you list them, the allowed senders are what keep others
out. Bodies over 64 KiB are refused.

* Adding a provider

A provider is one file in =src/telephony/= that implements the
=VentureTelephonyConnector= interface and ends with
=VENTURE_TELEPHONY_PROVIDER(its_get_type)=; nothing else names it. It
declares what it can do (click-to-call, call events, SMS, MMS, delivery
status, a signed webhook), its credential fields, how to place a call or
send a text through the line's pinned address, and how to read its
webhook into events. Requests go only to the line's own address, with no
redirects, no retries and a 15-second timeout, and refusals are mapped to
plain words that never repeat a secret. =tests/test-telephony.c= defines a
third, signed provider the same way.

* What is from public documentation and needs confirming

There is no live 3CX or VoIP.ms account behind this work. Both adapters
were written from the providers' public API documentation and tested
against fakes and the fixtures in =tests/fixtures/telephony/=. Each
adapter keeps its field names in one table at the top of its file, so a
correction is one line. Confirm these against a live account before
relying on them:

| Provider | What | Where in Venture |
|----------+------+------------------|
| 3CX | =POST /connect/token= with =grant_type=client_credentials=, =client_id=, =client_secret=; =access_token= in the answer | =token()= in =venture-telephony-3cx.c= |
| 3CX | =POST /callcontrol/{extension}/makecall= with ={"destination": ...}=; =finalstatus=, =reasontext=, =result.callid= in the answer; that the extension's phone rings first | =click_to_call()= |
| 3CX | The call journal's variable names (=CallID=, =CallType= with =Inbound=, =Outbound=, =Missed=, =Notanswered=, =Number=, =Agent=, =Duration= as hh:mm:ss, =CallStartTimeUTC=, =RecordingUrl=) and whether the template can post JSON | =journal_fields= and =call_types= |
| VoIP.ms | =POST /api/v1/rest.php= with =api_username=, =api_password=, =method= =sendSMS= or =sendMMS=, =did=, =dst=, =message=, =media1= to =media3=; =status= and =sms= or =mms= in the answer | =send_message()= in =venture-telephony-voipms.c= |
| VoIP.ms | The error statuses (=invalid_dst=, =invalid_did=, =invalid_credentials=, =ip_not_enabled=, =sms_toolong=, ...) | =refusals= |

* Storage and upgrade

Paired migrations =000950_telephony_lines_and_texts= (lines, extensions,
texts and the organization's three settings), =000951_telephony_call_inbox=
(the inbox flag on call activities) and =000952_telephony_text_consent=
(the number on consent evidence and a campaign's channel). Everything starts
empty and off: no line, no extension, no text, recording links off, every
existing campaign an email one and every existing call unchanged.
=tests/test-telephony.c= covers the registry, both adapters against their
fakes and fixtures, extension-first click-to-call, call logging and replay,
matching and missed calls, sending, receiving, the signed delivery receipt,
STOP/HELP/START, consent refusal, quiet hours across time zones, text steps,
text campaigns and that no secret reaches a record, an error or a log;
=tests/test-auth.c= covers every route.
