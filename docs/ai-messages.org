#+title: AI on messages
#+description: Drafted replies, routing, auto-reply rules, call summaries and follow-ups -- each approved by a person unless its rule sends on its own, and every send through the channel's own consent and quiet hours

The =ai_messages= module reads what customers send, drafts answers, decides
who should answer, answers on its own only where you told it to, and keeps a
log of all of it. It needs =inbox= ([[file:inbox.org][Conversations]]); it asks
the AI only when the organization's AI is set up ([[file:ai-organizations.org][Organization AI settings]]).

Open a conversation: the *AI on messages* card sits above the reply box.
The settings are at =/organizations/ID/settings/ai-messages=, linked from
the card and from the organization's page.

* The defaults

| What                    | Default                                                        |
|-------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------|
| AI messages             | On; the switch is per organization                             |
| Drafting and summaries  | Only where the organization's AI is configured                 |
| Every AI message        | A draft a person approves                                      |
| Auto-send on a rule     | Off                                                            |
| Auto-reply rules        | None                                                           |
| Business hours          | None, so no after-hours replies                                |
| Routes                  | None; unassigned conversations go through the lead routing rules |

* When a message comes in

A text, an email, a web chat message or a voicemail with a transcript is
read once, after it is saved (never while the phone system or mail sync
is still waiting):

1. *Intent*: booking, quote, question, complaint, cancellation or other.
   Anything else the AI answers becomes "other".
2. *Details*: name, phone, address, the date they asked for, the product
   or service. A detail is kept only if the message itself says it -- a
   phone number by its digits, anything else word for word. What the AI
   guessed is dropped.
3. *A reply draft* in the channel they used (a voicemail is answered by
   text, or by email when there is no number).
4. *Routing*: a conversation nobody owns goes to the person or team you
   chose for its intent; with no such route, the [[file:leads.org][lead routing rules]]
   decide, and they may test =intent= and =channel= like any other field.
   A conversation somebody already owns keeps its owner.

The customer's words are given to the AI as data, with the instruction
never to follow anything written in them, and with no tools to act with.

* Auto-reply rules

Each rule has:

- *When*: a message outside your business hours, or a missed call;
- *Answer by*: text, email, web chat, or the channel they used;
- *Words*: your own, or an AI draft;
- *Send without asking me*: off unless you tick it.

Business hours are days and times in the organization's time zone, for
example =Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00; Sat 10:00-14:00=. The first active rule that
applies answers, at most once per person per rule in 12 hours. A rule with
its own words works even without AI.

* Approving

Drafts wait at the top of the card, with why they were made, the intent
and details, the rule, and which provider and model wrote them. Edit the
words if you like, then *Approve and send*, or *Discard*. A decided message
cannot be sent again. Viewers can read the card but not approve.

*Draft a reply* asks for a new draft for their latest message; *Draft a
follow-up* (on the card, on a contact and on a deal) drafts a follow-up and
suggests a next step.

* What the AI never does

- *It never sends around consent or quiet hours.* Every send -- approved or
  automatic -- is the conversation's reply: the outbox for email, the one
  text send path for texts. Without their agreement to texts the send is
  refused; during their quiet hours it is held and goes when they end. The
  AI message says which.
- *It never invents prices, offers or dates.* Replies and follow-ups go
  through the grounding check of [[file:email-design.org][designed email]]: a sentence naming a
  price, an amount, a percentage, a date or "free" that is in neither the
  message nor the records is removed and listed. A follow-up with nothing
  left is refused.
- *It never summarises nothing.* A call with a transcript (given when it
  was logged, or sent by the phone system with recording links switched on)
  is summarised from it; a call without one gets "No transcript". Missed
  calls have nothing to summarise.

* The log

Every AI message is its own record: the draft or summary, the rule, the
provider and model, the reason, who approved it (or =auto=), when, and what
the send path said (sent, held for quiet hours, or refused and why). It is
shown on the conversation and returned by the API. The message the customer
receives is the channel's own record, written as the AI's with the approver
in the [[file:ai.org][audit trail]]:

#+begin_src sh
venturectl list audit_entry actor_kind__eq=ai target_type__eq=text_message
#+end_src

AI messages, rules and routes are written only by this module, never by
the generic record API.

* The switch

*Switch all AI messages off* on the settings page stops every reading,
draft, summary, auto-reply and send for the organization, including the
approval of drafts made before. The card and the settings page say so.

* API

| Method | Path                                         | Role   | Does                                          |
|--------+----------------------------------------------+--------+-----------------------------------------------|
| GET    | =/api/v1/conversations/:id/ai-messages=      | viewer | The contact's AI messages and whether it is on |
| POST   | =/api/v1/conversations/:id/ai-draft=         | editor | A new draft for their latest message           |
| POST   | =/api/v1/ai-messages/:id/approve=            | editor | ={"body": "..."}= optional; sends              |
| POST   | =/api/v1/ai-messages/:id/discard=            | editor | Discards a draft                               |
| POST   | =/api/v1/contacts/:id/ai-follow-up=          | editor | ={"channel": "text", "instruction": "..."}=    |
| POST   | =/api/v1/deals/:id/ai-follow-up=             | editor | The same, from a deal                          |

The settings page is an owner's or administrator's.

* Limits

- A message is read up to 6,000 characters, a transcript up to 24,000.
- A reply is at most 2,000 characters; a detail at most 200.
- One reading, one draft and one summary per message.
- Cold email follow-ups (to someone who never emailed) go through the
  outbox like any other single email; marketing consent applies to
  campaigns, not to these.
- Settings live on the organization and on the rules; a shared settings
  screen comes later.

tables and the organization's =ai_messages_off= and =business_hours=; all

switch, organization isolation and the pages; =test-auth= the sign-in and
role refusals of every route. No test calls a real AI.
