#+title: Progress invoicing, retainers and retention
#+description: Invoice remaining contract value and hold liability

An accepted quote with =billing-mode=progress= does not issue the full
invoice. =VentureProgressService= invoices a percent or amount of the original
total and refuses anything beyond the remaining contract value. Retainers
debit Cash and credit a configured liability, then release into income.
Retention is held as a liability against AR and released the same way.

Equal-percentage instalments receive distinct invoice numbers. Explicit progress amounts must use the contract currency, and failed currency arithmetic rolls back releases.

* Customer retainers through the shared action forms

A company's =Collect customer retainer= action records cash already received
against an active liability account in the same organization. Supply an
explicit currency with the amount. It does not charge a provider. The
resulting =customer_retainer= offers =Release earned retainer= to recognize
an earned portion as income; it neither issues nor settles an invoice.
Both actions require organization finance, owner or administrator authority
and the quotes module. The service owns the transaction and accounting
consent, so these actions cannot be staged as a generic record change.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl act company 1 collect_retainer 'amount=250 USD' liability_account_id=12
venturectl act customer_retainer 1 release 'amount=100 USD'
venturectl act customer_retainer 1 release 'amount=150 USD'
#+end_src

Each explicit collection represents a separate cash receipt. Each release
reduces the remaining liability; an over-release or a different currency is
refused and leaves the journal and remaining balance unchanged. Do not
record the same cash again as an ordinary invoice receipt. Linking the
retainer to a project retains traceability, but invoice-billed project
margin does not count this earned ledger income as a project invoice.
