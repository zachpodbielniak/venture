#+title: Durable follow-up sequences

* Enrollment and exits

The =sequences= module requires =crm=. Its five records are =sequence=,
=sequence_step=, =sequence_enrollment=, =sequence_delivery= and =suppression=.
Their fields drive the ordinary record surfaces. A sequence belongs to one
organization; its contacts, steps and optional deal must belong there too.
The optional =lead_id= is an external identifier, with no dependency on a
lead module.

=VentureSequenceService= owns enrollment and the database save hook routes
ordinary enrollment creation through it, including approved assistant writes.
Active and paused enrollment duplicates are refused. Suppressed addresses
cannot enroll. Addresses are trimmed and ASCII case-folded, unique per
organization. Suppression records are retained.

A sequence declares its IANA timezone, a same-day send window with an
inclusive start hour and exclusive end hour, and comma-separated ISO weekdays
(1 is Monday, 7 Sunday). Calendar-day delays use that timezone; hour delays
are elapsed hours. The stored next-run timestamp is UTC. An enrollment keeps
its reason and the actor who enrolled it.

An inbound interaction exits matching reply-sensitive enrollments; a meeting
can meet a meeting goal. A deal changing to won exits matching enrollments
without relying on a pipeline service. Suppression stops the email's
enrollments regardless of their unsubscribe switch. Each exit retains the
current step and an exit reason and cancels pending deliveries in the same
transaction. Already sent messages remain evidence.

* Mail adapter seam

A mail adapter picks up =sequence_delivery= rows with =channel=email= and
=state=pending=, sends, and sets state/external id. The sequence engine does
not send mail or depend on a mail implementation. The adapter must recheck
pending state before sending, since an exit can cancel a queued delivery.
