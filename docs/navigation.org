* What this is

The sidebar is grouped by the question a page answers, not by the module that owns it. An owner opening VENTURE asks what came in, what went out, who the customers are, who needs help and what is growing -- and then, less often, where the money sits and what the books say. The first headings after the overview are those, in that order. Everything else (the business itself, thinking, code, the factory, the system) keeps the heading it always had, after them.

Every heading after the overview folds. The group holding the page you are on is drawn open; the groups a person opened stay open from page to page (the sidebar's own script remembers them in the browser); the rest are one line each. Seventy rows at once was a list nobody read; a dozen headings is a map. Folding is =<details>=, so it works with scripting off.

This is grouping and labels only. Every page keeps its URL; nothing is moved or redirected; a module that is off hides its rows exactly as before; the role each page asks for is unchanged.

* The section map

| Heading   | Question               | Pages                                                                                                                                              |
|-----------+------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Money in  | What came in?          | Sales, Invoices, New invoice, New quote, Payments, Allocations, Customer credits, Refunds, Collections, Subscriptions, Sales orders                |
| Money out | What went out?         | Expenses, Pay bills, Purchasing, Claims, Payroll, Recurring                                                                                        |
| Customers | Who are the customers? | Companies, Contacts, My day, Duplicates                                                                                                            |
| Support   | Who needs help?        | Tickets, Sprints, Knowledge                                                                                                                        |
| Growth    | What is growing?       | Sales board, Deals, Campaigns, Newsletters, Posts                                                                                                  |
| Bank      | Where is the money?    | Bank feeds, Money calendar                                                                                                                         |
| Books     | What do the books say? | Books, Accounts, Journals, Journal lines, Tax, Tax filings, Fiscal years, Period close, Budgets, Owner equity, Group, Capture inbox, Setup, Cutover |

A page is listed under one question only. Bank and Books are the money that is neither in nor out: the old "Money" heading (accounts, tax, journals, fiscal years, the money calendar) and the "Accounting" heading are folded into them, so the accounting lives in one place. The headings that stay as they were, in the order they are drawn: Overview (Dashboard, Dashboards, Reports) before the questions; then Business, Thinking, Code, Factory and System. A heading the regrouping emptied -- Relations, Activities, Sales pipelines, Invoicing, Quotes, Money, Accounting -- is no longer drawn.

Pages that have not yet been filed under a question are drawn under the heading their row in the link table carries, so a module added later is never hidden: it appears where it always would have until a row is appended to the section that answers its question.

The record pages read the same map: a contact's page is headed "Customers / Contacts", and the group it belongs to is the one drawn open.

* How it is built

The link table (=venture_web_navigation()=) is unchanged and stays in the order it was written -- its URLs, labels, icons, module gates and the heading each row carries. The questions are a second table over it, =venture_web_navigation_sections()=: each section is a heading and the paths it gathers, in the order they are drawn. Drawing the sidebar walks the link table once; a row a section has claimed is drawn under its question rather than in place, and the questions are drawn together after the first heading. Each heading after the first is a =details.nav-group= whose summary keeps the =nav-section= class, so it is styled like the heading that does not fold.

A section's heading follows the first of its rows actually shown, the same rule as before, so a question every one of whose modules is off leaves no heading over nothing. The command palette (Ctrl+K) and the assistant's page context read the link table directly and are unaffected.

* Tests

=test-plugin= asserts each section's membership, that every path a section gathers is a row in the link table, that no path is gathered twice, and that no row is orphaned -- every row is either under a question or under a heading it inherits. =test-auth= renders the sidebar as an administrator and checks the headings appear in order after Overview, that a gathered page is drawn once and under its question, that the emptied headings are gone, that the group holding the current page is the one drawn open, and that switching a module off removes its row and leaves the question's heading in place. The anonymous-route test is untouched: it walks the same link table, so every page still answers a redirect to the sign-in page when nobody is signed in.
