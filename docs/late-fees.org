* Late fees

The =late_fees= module requires =dunning= and =receivables=. It is *off by
default*: until an owner switches it on, nothing is proposed, charged or
mentioned to a customer. Its three records are field tables, so the same
declarations produce the tables, the forms, the REST resources, the CLI
commands, the assistant's tools and the audit entries.

| Record | Meaning |
|--------+---------|
| =late_fee_setting= | One per organization: on/off, =auto_apply= (explicit opt-in), the default schedule, =enabled_at=, and the organization-wide pause |
| =late_fee_schedule= | A named, ordered list of tiers ("Residential", "Commercial net 30") with grace days, a minimum balance and schedule-level caps |
| =late_fee= | One occurrence of one tier for one invoice and every decision about it; written only by the sweep and the fee actions |

*Late-fee limits vary by jurisdiction.* The schedule supports a cap per tier,
a cap as a percent of the original invoice and an absolute maximum; set them
for your location, and say which rule the schedule follows in its =notes=.

** Which schedule

The invoice's =late_fee_schedule_id=, else its customer's, else the first
schedule whose =invoice_types= (comma separated) names the invoice's
=invoice_type=, else the setting's =default_schedule_id=. =late_fee_exempt=
on the invoice or the customer means no fee at all.

** Tiers

=tiers= is a JSON array, judged at the save; at most 20.

#+begin_src json
[{"day": 15, "type": "flat", "amount": "25.00 USD"},
 {"day": 30, "type": "percent_open", "percent": "1.5"},
 {"after": 30, "every": 30, "type": "percent_open", "percent": "1.5", "round": "half_even"}]
#+end_src

With =grace_days= 14 and =max_percent= "10" this is the example from the
issue: days 1-14 grace; on day 15 $25 once; on day 30 1.5% of the open
balance; every 30 days after, 1.5% again; never more than 10% of the original
invoice.

| Member | Meaning |
|--------+---------|
| =day= | The first occurrence, in days late (1-365); day N is the due date plus N calendar days, UTC |
| =every= | Recurring: again every N days after =day= |
| =after= | With =every= and no =day=: "every N days after day M", first on M + N |
| =times= | At most this many occurrences |
| =type= | =flat= (=amount=), =percent_open= (of the open balance, fees excluded), =percent_original= (of the original invoice), =markup= (a percent added to everything outstanding, fees included, so it compounds) |
| =percent= | Decimal *text* ("1.5", at most four places) or a JSON integer; a JSON fraction is refused, never read through a double |
| =amount=, =cap= | Money with its currency, "25.00 USD"; =cap= is the most this tier charges on one invoice |
| =round= | =half_even= (default), =up=, =down= or =whole= (whole currency units, half to even) |
| =conditions= | All must hold: =balance_above=, =balance_below= (money), =customer_tags= (any of the customer's comma-separated tags; =customer_groups= is the same), =invoice_types=, =product_ids= (a service line on the invoice), =currency=, =min_prior_late= (other invoices of the customer due earlier and paid late or still overdue), =skip_if_promise= (a reminder pause, or a held, disputed or promised collection case) |

A fee is computed in integer minor units, once, by the tier's rule, then cut
by the room left under the tier cap, the schedule's =max_total= and its
=max_percent= of the original invoice. A fee a cap reduces to nothing is
recorded =capped=. A flat amount or a cap in another currency is not charged:
cross-currency is refused, never converted.

** Identity and replays

An occurrence is =(invoice, schedule, trigger, day)=, pinned by the
per-organization unique =occurrence_key= - the trigger, not the tier's
position, so inserting a tier never makes an occurrence chargeable twice.
Rerunning the sweep for the same day writes nothing, and a skipped, waived,
cancelled or capped occurrence never comes back.

Occurrences before =enabled_at= (set when late fees are switched on) or the
schedule's =adopted_at= are never charged: switching late fees on over an
existing ledger does not bill every old invoice for every day it passed.
Moving an invoice or customer to another schedule keeps every fee already
charged; the new schedule only governs days after the last day the old one
decided, and the old schedule's pending fees are cancelled with reason
=schedule changed=. The move itself is in the audit log like any field.

* The sweep: one stage of the dunning sweep

There is no second sweep. =venture_dunning_service_sweep_detailed()= (the
REST action, =venturectl dunning sweep=, the assistant's tool and the
automation handler) runs a late-fee stage for each invoice it visits, before
the reminder stage and in its own transaction. With late fees on it visits
every issued invoice due before today, not only those with a reminder step
due. The answer carries a =late_fees= object (=enabled=, =proposed=,
=applied=, =awaiting_notice=, =capped=, =cancelled=, =failed=), and a dry run
adds =stage: late_fee= entries to its =plan=.

For each due occurrence the stage, in order: skips it while a pause holds;
cancels pending fees of a settled, exempt or reassigned invoice; checks the
tier's conditions and the schedule's minimum balance; computes the amount and
the caps; then

1. *Notice.* No fee proceeds unless a reminder that stated it was *sent*
   before its day, or the owner explicitly skipped the notice
   (=skip_notice=). Otherwise it waits as =awaiting_notice=, visible in the
   approval queue.
2. *Approval.* The fee is =proposed=. Only with =auto_apply= does the sweep
   approve it itself, one fee per transaction after the proposal committed.

A fee is never more than the amount the notice stated; a percent fee
recomputed on its day can only be lower.

** Advance notice in reminders

When a reminder renders and a fee would follow, the sentence "A $25.00 late
fee will be added on 2026-01-25." is placed at ={late_fee_notice}= in the
template, or appended to the text and HTML bodies of a template that does not
name it - a fee must never follow a reminder that did not state it. The fee
row records the reminder (=notice_event_id=) and the amount stated
(=noticed_amount=, status =scheduled=). While late fees are off, paused or
exempt no fee wording reaches the customer, and a fee already proposed is not
re-announced. Each tier's fee needs a reminder step before its day: a policy
with steps at 7, 25, 40, 55 and 85 days announces fees on days 15, 30, 45,
60 and 90.

* Pausing

| Scope | Fields |
|-------+--------|
| An invoice | =late_fees_paused=, =late_fees_paused_until=, =late_fees_pause_reason= on the invoice |
| A customer | the same three on the company; covers all of its invoices |
| The organization | =paused=, =paused_until=, =pause_reason= on =late_fee_setting= |

=paused_until= is the first day fees may apply again; empty while paused is
indefinitely. A pause is not a decision about any fee: nothing is proposed,
added or announced while it holds, and when it ends the due occurrences
proceed - each still needs its notice. The invoice page, its fee timeline,
the Late fees page and the invoice's timeline say "late fees paused for this
customer until <date>" wherever a fee would otherwise apply.

* Decisions

| Action | On | Does |
|--------+----+------|
| =approve= | a proposed =late_fee= | Adds it through =venture_settlement_service_add_late_fee()=: a separate =late_fee= invoice event, receivables debited, income credited, dated the fee's day (or the invoice's latest event when later) |
| =skip= | a fee not yet added | Records the decision not to charge; nothing reaches the balance |
| =skip_notice= | a =scheduled= or =awaiting_notice= fee | The owner lets it proceed without the advance notice |
| =waive= | an applied fee | =reason= required, optional =note= to the customer; a =late_fee_waiver= credit (income debited, receivables credited) applied to the invoice up to its open balance, any rest left as the customer's credit; the note is queued through the mail outbox, keyed by the fee |
| =decide= | type-level on =late_fee= | =ids= (comma separated) and =decision= =approve= or =skip=: each its own transaction, one refusal does not undo the others |
| =pause_late_fees=, =resume_late_fees= | =invoice=, =company=, =late_fee_setting= | =until= and =reason=; an ordinary audited save |
| =late_fee_preview= | =invoice= | The full timeline, writing nothing; the answer is on the returned record's =note= |

All writing actions are stageable, so =?stage=1=, =venturectl --stage act= and
the assistant (its generated tools =venture_late_fee_waive=,
=venture_company_pause_late_fees=, ...) go through the existing staged-change
approval. "Pause late fees for this customer" and "waive this fee" are those
two tools; under the default =confirm_writes= policy a person approves each.

The invoice's lines are never edited. A void is refused while an unwaived
late fee stands ("Waive the invoice's late fees before voiding it"). Late
fee history cannot be deleted, restored or purged, and a =late_fee= cannot be
written by any generic writer.

* Preview

=venture_late_fee_service_preview()=, the =late_fee_preview= action and
=/invoices/<id>/late-fees= show "what this schedule would charge" on any
invoice: every occurrence up to 365 days late with its date, tier, basis,
exact amount, state (a recorded fee's status, or =upcoming=, =due=,
=capped=, =skipped_condition= with the reason, =before_start=) and the
notice state, assuming today's balance. The page at =/late-fees= shows the
settings, every schedule's tiers in words, the organization pause, the paused
customers and invoices, and links to the approval queue at
=/late-fees/queue= (bulk approve or skip with checkboxes). Schedules and
settings are edited through the generated forms. Owner or administrator only.

* Where fees show

- The invoice page's Late fees panel: each fee, its state and basis, the
  waive form on an applied one, and the pause form.
- The customer statement: "Late fee #N" debits and "Late fee waiver #N"
  credits; the balance and aging include them through
  =venture_settlement_service_invoice_balance()=.
- The invoice's and the customer's timeline: "late fee $25.00 applied
  (tier 1, 2026-01-25)", "... proposed for approval", "... announced for",
  "... waived: goodwill", and for an invoice a computed "next: a $25.00 late
  fee will be added on ..." or the pause in force.
- The =late_fees= report: per currency, fees applied (by the fee's day),
  waived (by the decision), collected (on invoices paid in the period) and
  awaiting approval or notice.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl create late_fee_schedule name=Residential grace_days=14 max_percent=10 \
  'tiers=[{"day":15,"type":"flat","amount":"25.00 USD"},{"day":30,"every":30,"type":"percent_open","percent":"1.5"}]'
venturectl create late_fee_setting enabled=true default_schedule_id=1
venturectl act invoice 42 late_fee_preview
venturectl dunning sweep as_of=2026-01-25 organization_id=1 dry_run=true
venturectl act late_fee 7 approve
venturectl act late_fee 0 decide organization_id=1 ids=7,8,9 decision=approve
venturectl act company 3 pause_late_fees until=2026-02-01 reason='promised by phone'
venturectl act late_fee 7 waive reason=goodwill note='We removed the fee.'
venturectl report late_fees all
#+end_src
