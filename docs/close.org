#+title: Accountant close workspace
#+description: Period close checklist, subledger tie-outs, workpapers and signoff

* A close belongs to one fiscal period

The =close= module requires =periods=, =ledger= and =statements=. It
suggests =banking=, =receivables=, =payables= and =assets=. It owns
=close_workspace=, =close_task=, =close_workpaper=, =close_discrepancy= and
=close_signoff=. Turning it off hides those records and the
=close_workspace= report together.

Open a workspace against an existing fiscal period. The service creates the
nine checklist items twice, once for the preparer and once for the reviewer:
bank reconciliation, AR control, AP control, suspense, tax, depreciation,
deferrals, trial-balance tie-out and subledger tie-out.

* Checks reuse the books you already keep

=venture_close_service_run_checks()= compares:

- unmatched =bank_transaction= rows in the period
- receivables aging outstanding against control account 1100
- payables aging outstanding against control account 2000
- suspense (code 9999 or a name containing "suspense")
- tax control 2100 (or the mapped =tax= account)
- scheduled, unposted =depreciation_entry= and =deferral_entry= rows
- the authoritative =trial_balance= report

Differences become =close_discrepancy= rows. An unexplained difference
refuses the run. Explain it, or link =correction_type= and =correction_id=
to the journal, bill or receipt that fixes it.

* Signoff, pack, complete, reopen

Every task must be done or waived. The preparer signs first, the reviewer
second. Complete then closes the fiscal period through
=VenturePeriodService=, so the ordinary close checklist, snapshots and
=periods.reopen= permission still apply. Reopening the workspace reopens
that period the same way. =venture_close_service_pack()= returns the
trial balance, AR/AP aging, bank reconciliation and statements as JSON.

Generic writes cannot mint a signoff or jump a workspace to =completed=.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl close open fiscal_period_id=1 currency=USD
venturectl close run 1
venturectl close sign 1 role=preparer
venturectl close sign 1 role=reviewer
venturectl close pack 1
venturectl close complete 1
venturectl close reopen 1
venturectl report close_workspace 2026-01 organization_id=1
#+end_src

Completion reruns the current accounting checks; cached tie-out flags cannot authorize closing changed books. Reopening retains historical signoffs but requires a new preparer and reviewer sequence. Generic writes cannot create or edit workspace evidence, and the checklist must contain every required kind for both roles; missing or duplicate tasks fail signoff. Unmatched transactions in a different currency fail reconciliation instead of being summed as book-currency minor units, and overflowing totals are refused.

The preparer and reviewer must be different authenticated accounts. For authenticated requests, signoff identity uses the account ID, so two API tokens owned by one person cannot supply both signatures. Trusted internal service calls without an authenticated principal use the explicit actor name.

* Completing the checklist through the ordinary record pages

Open a fiscal period and select =Open close workspace=. The generated
=close_workspace= detail page offers =Run close checks=, =Sign close=,
=Complete close= and =Reopen close=. Each related =close_task= page offers
=Complete check= and =Waive check=, both requiring notes. A discrepancy's
=Explain discrepancy= action retains the explanation before signoff.
These are registered =VentureAction= operations: the web form, REST API,
CLI and enabled assistant/MCP action surfaces call the same close service.
No separate handler constructs checklist or signature records.

Authenticated users need organization owner, administrator or finance
membership and the editor minimum. A viewer cannot complete a task. The
close module must be enabled. The service performs multiple writes in one
transaction, so these actions cannot be staged. A signed-off or completed
checklist cannot be changed through its completion/waiver action: reopen
the workspace, then complete the new review cycle. Currency, when supplied
at opening, must be a supported ISO code.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl act fiscal_period 1 open_close currency=USD
venturectl list close_task workspace_id=1
venturectl act close_task 1 complete 'notes=Compared the control balance with the statement'
venturectl act close_task 2 waive 'notes=No assets exist in this organization'
venturectl act close_discrepancy 1 explain 'explanation=Reviewed timing difference; supporting workpaper attached'
venturectl act close_workspace 1 run_checks
venturectl act close_workspace 1 sign role=preparer
# A different authenticated account signs as reviewer.
venturectl act close_workspace 1 sign role=reviewer
venturectl act close_workspace 1 complete
#+end_src
