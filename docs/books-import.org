#+title: Books migration
#+description: A whole Zoho Books backup with its history, replayed through VENTURE's services and reconciled

* What it brings

The =books_import= module requires =cutover=, =receivables= and
=payables=. It registers =books_import= (a batch) and =books_import_row=
(one row per source document, or per record it became), and
=VentureBooksImportService= owns preview, import, reconcile, activate,
rollback preflight and rollback. Generic writes to either record are
refused naming the service.

The [[file:cutover.org][cutover]] brings balances: what was open at a cutoff.
A books migration brings the history: every contact, every invoice since
the source's first day (paid, open, draft and void), every customer and
vendor payment, bill, expense, credit note, refund, deposit, transfer and
manual journal. The day-one balances enter through an ordinary cutover
batch at the start date; everything after is replayed in date order
through the service that owns it, so the ledger is built by VENTURE, not
summarised into journals, and aging, statements, customer history and the
operational reports all show the business as it ran.

Source: =zoho_books=, the standard backup (Settings > Data Backup, a zip of
one CSV per object). QuickBooks and Xero are not covered yet.

* The manifest

#+begin_src json
{"source": "zoho_books",
 "start": "2024-12-01",
 "export_date": "2026-10-24",
 "currency": "USD",
 "opening": [{"account": "Checking", "debit": "250.00"},
             {"account": "Opening Balance Adjustments", "credit": "250.00"}],
 "bank_charges_account": "Bank Fees and Charges",
 "purchase_tax_account": "Tax Paid Expense",
 "discount_account": "Discount",
 "adjustment_account": "Other Charges",
 "accounts": {"Sales": "4000"},
 "contact_details": false,
 "files": {"Invoice.csv": "...csv text...", "Bill.csv": "..."}}
#+end_src

| Member | Meaning |
|--------+---------|
| =start= | the source's first day; every document must be on or after it, and the opening balances enter here |
| =export_date= | the last day the backup covers; reconcile checks it, and a document after it is a problem |
| =opening= | the day-one balances, one row per Zoho account with a debit or a credit; they must balance |
| =bank_charges_account= | where Zoho booked a payment's bank charges (Zoho's setting; the backup does not say) |
| =purchase_tax_account= | where Zoho booked tax on bills and expenses |
| =discount_account= | where Zoho booked line discounts |
| =adjustment_account= | an invoice adjustment with no account of its own |
| =accounts= | Zoho account name to VENTURE code, for an account matched some other way than by name |
| =contact_details= | bring contacts' email and phone; off by default (see Mail) |
| =files= | backup file name to its CSV text |

The opening rows come from the owner: Zoho keeps opening balances in its
settings, not in the backup. A Zoho account the chart names is matched, in
order, to the receivables or payables control account for Zoho's control
types, the code =accounts= maps it to, the one account of the same name
(case does not matter), or a new account in its kind's range. Two accounts
of one name are a problem to resolve by mapping. A contact is matched by
external id (=zoho-c-ID= for customers, =zoho-v-ID= for vendors, the ids an
opening cutover also uses), then by name, then created as a customer or a
supplier.

* How each document is replayed

| Zoho | VENTURE |
|------+---------|
| invoice | draft with its lines, each naming its income account, then issued on its date; a line discount keeps its own discount account; draft stays a draft; void is issued and voided |
| customer payment | receipt applied to its invoices, marked as bookkeeping; the deposit account and bank charges are a dated journal from the cash account |
| payment before its invoice | the receipt's credit, applied when the invoice is issued |
| bill | draft with a line per item and one for purchase tax, approved on its date; draft and void as for invoices |
| bill adjustment (Zoho's rounding cent) | carried on the tax line, then a dated journal moves it to its account |
| vendor payment | payment applied to its bills; paid-through account as a dated journal; before its bill, the payment's credit |
| expense | an expense record naming its account and paid-through account; its tax is a second expense on the purchase tax account |
| deposit, transfer | the capital service: an owner contribution, a draw or a transfer |
| manual journal | a journal of the batch on its date |
| credit note, refund, vendor credit, vendor refund | the credit, its application or refund, and a dated journal to the line accounts |

Where VENTURE's own posting differs from Zoho's -- a receipt lands in the
organization's cash account, a credit note debits its income account --
the document posts the ordinary way and a journal linked to the batch moves
the leg on the same date. Each is one document's correction, never a
summary. These need the per-line income and discount accounts of invoice
lines and the expense account of an expense (migration =000988=).

Not imported, and listed on every preview with the reason: Zoho's activity
log, budgets, tax settings, recurring profiles (their past documents are
imported; set the schedules up again), fixed assets, projects, tasks,
timesheets, quotes, sales and purchase orders, items and contact persons,
and the files with no VENTURE record. Each is a line, not a refusal.

* Preview, import, reconcile, activate

Preview parses the whole backup without writing a document: the counts per
object, the accounts and companies to be created, every located problem
(=Invoice.csv row 12: account "Consulting" is not in Chart_of_Accounts.csv=)
and every file left behind. A manifest that cannot be read at all -- an
unknown source, no start, a file that is not CSV -- is refused and no batch
is created. Import re-runs the same plan and refuses before its first write
while any problem remains; it is one transaction, and a second import of an
imported batch is a no-op. A source document another live batch imported
is a problem: the same history is never counted twice.

Reconcile rebuilds Zoho's own ledger from the backup -- the postings Zoho
made for each document -- and compares every account of the organization
with it at every month end from the start and at the export date, and each
month's invoices, bills, customer and vendor payments and expenses by count
and total with what VENTURE holds. An account Zoho never named (the cash
account a receipt passed through) must be back at zero. The report is
appended, with =PASS= and =FAIL= lines, the trial balance at the last date
and =Result: reconciled= or =FAILED=; a reconciled batch that no longer
ties returns to =imported=. VENTURE does not close years into retained
earnings, so its trial balance carries every year's income and expense;
Zoho's shows earlier years as retained earnings.

Activate re-runs reconcile, reconciles and activates the opening cutover
(which closes periods ending at the start), sets the organization's
=pnl-basis= to =ledger= -- its books are now the ledger, so its profit and
loss page and dashboard card read the posted income and expense accounts,
accrual, as the source system did, rather than sale and expense records --
and sets =active=. An active batch is the books.

* Rollback

Allowed before activation. One transaction, dated on the rollback day: the
cash on every imported document is refunded, every invoice and bill is
voided and its number takes the suffix =-rbID=, drafts are removed, the
batch's own journals, expenses and capital postings are reversed, and the
opening cutover is rolled back. Companies and accounts stay for a
corrected batch. A credit note that was applied or refunded is a blocker:
an applied credit cannot be unapplied.

The ledger keeps the rolled-back history at its source dates, with the
reversal on the rollback day. A corrected batch of the same books is
reconciled net of it: each rolled-back batch's source ledger, from its
dates until its rollback day.

* Mail and collections

Nothing is sent during an import. Receipts are marked as bookkeeping, so
no customer is thanked for money paid years ago; issuing an invoice sends
nothing. Imported invoices carry =dunning-disabled= and =late-fee-exempt=,
and imported companies =dunning-opt-out= and =late-fee-exempt=: an overdue
Zoho invoice must not draw a reminder or a fee the day the books arrive.
Turn collections back on per customer when the owner decides. Email and
phone come over only with =contact_details=: prove the installation's mail
goes somewhere safe first.

* Surfaces

| Surface | Call |
|---------+------|
| REST | =POST /api/v1/books_imports/preview= with the manifest, into the selected legal entity |
| REST | =POST /api/v1/books_imports/:id/import=, =/reconcile=, =/activate=, =/rollback_preflight=, =/rollback= |
| CLI | =venturectl books preview DIR start=YYYY-MM-DD [export_date=...] [opening=@FILE] [name=value ...]= reads every CSV in DIR |
| CLI | =venturectl books import ID=, =books reconcile ID=, =books activate ID=, =books rollback ID= |

All require an editor and the module and answer 401 unauthenticated
(=test-auth=). Reconcile on a large history takes minutes; the action keeps
running if the client gives up, and the batch's report shows the result.

* Verification

=test-books-import= replays an invented company's backup in Zoho's CSV
shapes -- a day-one bank balance, paid and open invoices with a discount
and a late fee, a draft and a void, payments with bank charges and a
clearing deposit account, bills with purchase tax and a rounding
adjustment, expenses with inclusive tax, an owner deposit and a transfer --
and checks every account to the cent, a mid-month balance, drafts, voids,
the collections flags and that no mail was queued; reconcile passing and
failing on drift; preview counts, refusals and located problems; idempotent
import; rollback to zero and a reconciled re-import; the same history
refused twice; and the generic-write refusal. =test-line-accounts= covers
the line and expense accounts.
