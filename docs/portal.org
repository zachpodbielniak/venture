#+title: Customer accounting portal
#+description: Isolated invoices, statements and pay

=VenturePortalService= issues a 256-bit token for one company. =GET
/portal/:token= lists that customer's invoices. Pay opens Stripe Checkout; only a verified provider completion records a receipt through =VentureSettlementService=. Revoke ends access without deleting history.
Unknown or revoked tokens return 404.

* Checkout

The customer page's Checkout action revalidates the invitation token and
invoice ownership, then opens the existing Stripe Checkout service for the
server-calculated outstanding balance. A missing Stripe configuration is an
explicit error. Only verified Stripe completion records a receipt; submitting
an amount to the portal does not pay an invoice.

* Invitations

=POST /api/v1/customer_portal/invite= accepts =company_id= and =email= for the active organization and requires editor access. Configure =server.base_url= as the public HTTPS URL and enable/configure transactional mail. Invitation creation and outbox enqueue share one transaction; the private link is stored in a sensitive mail-body field and is delivered only by the mail transport. Neither the access token nor the private body appears in generic API, UI, audit or AI output. Unknown, revoked and module-disabled invitations return 404.

* Subscriptions

When billing is on, the portal page also lists the invited customer's own
subscriptions: plan and price (and seats, for a per-seat price), its state,
the next renewal date and what that invoice will be -- from
=venture_billing_service_next_invoice()=, the same figure the operator's
page and the price-change notice give, read "plus usage" on a metered
price because the period's usage is only known when it closes -- any
switch due at renewal, and
the invoices it has issued with what is still owed on each.

A live subscription (trialing, active or overdue, and not already ending)
offers two things, each a form posted to
=POST /portal/:token/subscriptions/:id=:

- *Switch plan* (=action=change=, =plan_price_id=, =when=renewal|now=) to any
  active price, in the same currency, of an active plan sold by the venture
  that sells the customer's current plan -- or of the shared plans, when
  that plan is shared. At renewal is the default; now prorates exactly as
  an operator's change does.
- *Cancel at renewal* (=action=cancel=): they keep it until the period ends
  and nothing more is invoiced.

A customer cannot pause, cancel immediately or change seats here. Both
actions go through =venture_portal_service_manage_subscription()=, which
re-checks the token (so a revoked link stops at once), then calls
=venture_billing_service_execute()= pinned to the version the subscription
was read at, so every service rule and the customer's price-change notice
apply. A subscription that is not the token's customer's -- another
company's, another organization's, deleted, or no such id -- is
=NOT_FOUND= (404), never =FORBIDDEN=, so a guessed id says nothing. With
billing off the section is absent and the route is 404. =test-portal=
covers the page, the offer list and every cross-customer attempt, and
=test-auth= the route without a valid token.
