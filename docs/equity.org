#+title: Owner equity, loans and transfers
#+description: Guided balance-sheet postings that refuse expense dumps

* Guided capital

The =equity= module requires =ledger= and =setup=. =VentureCapitalService=
posts a balanced journal through the posting service for one of:

| Kind | Debit | Credit |
| contribution | cash (1000) | owner's equity (3000) |
| draw | owner's draw (3100) | cash (1000) |
| loan_proceed | cash (1000) | notes payable (2500) |
| loan_payment | notes payable (2500) | cash (1000) |
| transfer | named cash | named cash |

Accounts come from =accounting_control_map=. Overrides must still be asset,
liability or equity: an expense or income account is refused as an unlabeled
expense dump. Generic writes of =equity_transaction= are refused.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl equity contribution '1000.00 USD' memo='Owner contribution'
venturectl equity draw '250.00 USD'
#+end_src

The web page is =/equity=.
