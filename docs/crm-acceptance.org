#+title: CRM to collection acceptance
#+description: Reproducible sales identity and project handoff checks

* Operator walkthrough

1. Enable Leads, CRM, Pipelines, Activities, Mail, Quotes, Projects and the
   invoicing/receivables dependencies. Set a lead assignment rule and a public
   lead form with duplicate policy =merge=.
2. Capture an inquiry twice using the same normalized email. Inspect one
   lead, its capture history and assigned owner. Qualify it and select its
   campaign. Public submissions cannot choose organization or owner.
3. Plan the qualification follow-up, then convert the lead with a deal.
   Inspect its retained company/contact/deal links. A newly created deal
   inherits the lead's owner, source and campaign; linking existing company
   or contact records does not rewrite those records' history. Conversion
   does not grant membership or a role to the owner label.
4. Respond through the ordinary transactional mail outbox, with a stable
   idempotency key. Sending the same queued identity again does not enqueue
   another message. Uncertain transport delivery still requires the existing
   explicit operator workflow; no exactly-once SMTP guarantee is implied.
5. Add priced deal lines and create the linked quote. Select progress billing,
   send it and record acceptance through the existing quote action.
6. Start the client project from the accepted quote. Plan a priced delivery,
   complete its ordinary ticket, record acceptance and invoice the slice.
   The project points to the same customer, deal and accepted agreement.
7. Collect the invoice through the settlement service. Verify the remaining
   invoice balance is zero and replaying delivery billing returns its existing
   invoice. Further contractual work remains available for later slices.

See [[file:leads.org][leads]], [[file:project-delivery.org][delivery]], and
[[file:accounting-acceptance.org][accounting reconciliation]] for the individual
contracts. Import adapters have their own preview/match/rollback tests; this
scenario starts at form capture and does not claim to qualify every importer.

* Executable evidence

=test-crm-acceptance= uses synthetic names and an in-memory log mail transport:
no external messages are sent. Two capture submissions normalize to one lead.
Conversion produces one company, one contact and one deal, retaining the
planned activity and history; replaying a stale conversion is refused. One
mail identity is delivered to the test transport once. A 1,000 USD agreement
produces a 400 USD accepted milestone invoice, whose balance falls from
40,000 minor units to zero on collection. Billing replay leaves exactly one
invoice and one payment. These are source-record assertions, not snapshots
of a page or a row count standing in for money.

The first complete handoff check failed because the newly converted deal's
owner was NULL instead of the assigned rep. Conversion now copies ownership
from its reloaded lead inside the same transaction.

The PostgreSQL helper creates and removes a unique schema. Use a disposable
database and never a production connection. This proves the scripted core
workflow; marketing consent, anonymous attribution, real mail delivery,
provider settlement and deployment parallel-use acceptance have separate
evidence requirements.
