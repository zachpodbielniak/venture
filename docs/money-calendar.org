* What it answers

Money out and money in, by day. The =money_calendar= module puts every
dated money event the other modules already know about on one grid:
recurring schedules expanded forward, approved bills on their due date,
issued invoices on theirs, the reminder steps a dunning policy will send,
pay runs not yet disbursed and tax packs not yet submitted. Each day and
each ISO week shows its net, per currency. Nothing here is a new record:
the calendar reads, it never writes, and a date is changed on the record it
belongs to, not by dragging it.

The module requires =receivables= and =payables= and suggests =recurring=,
=dunning=, =payroll= and =tax_filing=; a source whose module is off simply
contributes nothing. Switching =money_calendar= off hides the report, the
page, the feed and the CLI verb together.

* Events

An event is a #VentureMoneyCalendarEvent: a kind, a calendar day, a
title, an amount as =VentureMoney=, a direction (=in= or =out=), the
record it points at (=vendor_bill:12=), the counterparty and venture where
the record has one, and two flags: =overdue= and whether it counts in the
net.

| Kind | Source | Day | Amount | Direction |
|------+--------+-----+--------+-----------|
| =recurring= | =recurring_schedule=, not paused, not a journal | each occurrence from the schedule's cycle index forward | the template: invoice or bill lines summed (quantity x unit price plus tax), an expense's amount | invoice in; bill and expense out |
| =bill= | =vendor_bill= approved or partially paid | =due_date= | the open balance from =VenturePayablesService= | out |
| =invoice= | =invoice= sent or partially paid | =due_at= | the open balance from =VentureSettlementService= | in |
| =dunning= | each policy step not yet recorded as a =dunning_event= | =due_at= plus the step's offset | the invoice's open balance, shown but not counted | in |
| =payroll= | =payroll_run= with status =imported= | =period_end= | net pay (until =net_disbursed=) and liabilities (until =tax_disbursed=), as two events | out |
| =tax= | =tax_filing= draft or reviewed | =period_end= | the pack's =tax= | out |

Recurring occurrences use the schedule's own IANA timezone, exactly as the
recurring sweep does: the start is moved into that zone and months, weeks,
days or years are added there, so a schedule anchored on the 31st lands on
28 February and returns to 31 March, and a local 23:30 in Auckland stays
on its day across the daylight-saving change instead of slipping to the
next one in UTC arithmetic. An occurrence the sweep has already generated
is not projected again; the document it produced appears in its own right.
Journal schedules have no direction and are not money.

Bills and invoices carry their *open balance*, so a partially paid invoice
shows what is still owed. Drafts, paid, void and disputed documents, and
invoices or customers switched out of dunning, are not on the calendar.
An item past due and still open is *carried to today* and flagged
=overdue=: the grid shows what must be paid, not what should have been. Its
anchor day, the original due date, is kept, so the feed's UID does not
change from one day to the next.

Pay runs record no pay date and tax packs record no filing deadline; both
use the end of the period they cover. That is an assumption, written here
so nobody mistakes it for a fact about the jurisdiction.

* Nets

Every day and every ISO week (=2026-W03=, on the ISO week-numbering year)
has one net per currency: money in less money out over the events that
count. A day with a USD invoice and a EUR bill has a USD net and a EUR
net, both shown, neither converted; the calendar never adds across
currencies. Reminders show the invoice balance on the day the reminder
goes out, but the invoice is already counted on its due date, so a
reminder is never in a net.

* The report

=money_calendar= is an ordinary registry report. It takes the period, or
=from= and =to= as an explicit inclusive span, and =as_of= for the day
overdue items are carried to (default now). =kind= keeps one kind and
refuses an unknown one; =customer_id= or =vendor_id= keep one
counterparty; =venture_id= and =currency= narrow further. Rows carry
=date=, =week=, =kind=, =title=, =counterparty=, =direction=, =amount=,
=currency=, =status= (=due=, =overdue= or =reminder=), =record= and the
=day_net= and =week_net= of that row's day and week. Metrics give the
event count and, per currency, money in, money out and the net over the
span.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl report money_calendar 2026-03
venturectl report money_calendar 2026 from=2026-03-01 to=2026-03-31 kind=bill
curl '.../api/v1/reports/money_calendar?period=this_month&as_of=2026-03-02&format=csv'
#+end_src

* The page

=/money/calendar= renders month, week and agenda views server-side, like
every other page: =view= is =month=, =week= or =agenda=, =period= any
period expression (=2026-03=, =this_week=, =2026-03-02..2026-03-08=), and
the filter form (=kind=, =company_id=, =venture_id=, =currency=) is
hx-boosted so a change re-renders in place. Month and week are a grid of
Monday-to-Sunday rows with each day's events and net, and the ISO week's
net closing each row; the agenda lists days under their week with both
nets. Every event links to its record at =/e/TYPE/ID=. There is no drag:
a due date is a fact on a record.

* The feed

=GET /money/calendar.ics= returns the same events, filtered the same way,
as all-day =VEVENT=s (=DTSTART;VALUE=DATE=, exclusive =DTEND= the next
day) with stable UIDs of the form
=money-KIND-TYPE-ID-ANCHOR@venture=, so a phone calendar can subscribe
and an overdue bill carried forward updates in place rather than
duplicating. The writer is the activities module's, so folding and
escaping are the bytes =/api/v1/activities.ics= already produces. It is
an export, not a synchronisation protocol, and it needs a viewer token
like the rest of the API.

* The CLI

#+begin_src sh
venturectl money calendar --from 2026-03-01 --to 2026-03-31
venturectl money calendar --from 2026-03-01 --to 2026-03-31 --kind bill --as-of 2026-03-02
venturectl -f json money calendar --from 2026-03-01 --to 2026-03-31
#+end_src

Prints the agenda: one heading per day with its week and net, one line
per event with kind, direction, amount and title, overdue and reminder
rows marked. =-f json= prints the report as the server sent it. =--from=
and =--to= are both required; an unknown kind is refused by the server.

* Extending

Sources implement the =VentureMoneyCalendarSource= interface
(=get_kind=, =get_module=, =expand=) and are held by a
=VentureMoneyCalendarExpander=; the default expander holds the six built-in
sources, and a plugin adds one with
=venture_money_calendar_expander_add()=, replacing any source of the same
kind. The report, the page, the feed and the CLI all read the expanded
array, so a new source appears everywhere at once. The =cash_forecast=
report of the =pnl= work reads the same records; once both have landed the
two should share this one expander.

* Verification

=test-money-calendar= covers month-end and daylight-saving expansion in the
schedule's timezone, paused, ended, generated and journal schedules,
bills and invoices at their open balance, overdue carry with the anchor
day kept, settled and draft exclusion, dunning steps against recorded
events, payroll net and liabilities, tax packs by status, the report's
rows and per-currency nets, from/to and kind options with the unknown-kind
refusal, module-off refusals for a source and for the whole module, the
feed's all-day events and stable UIDs, the three page views with filters
and record links, the feed route's content type, and the CLI verb with
its usage refusals.
