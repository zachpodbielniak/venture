#+title: Conversations: one inbox for every customer
#+description: Email, texts, calls, voicemail and web chat in one thread per contact, with an owner, a status and replies in the channel the customer used

The =inbox= module gives every contact one conversation: their email, their
texts, their calls (missed ones too), their voicemail and their web chat,
oldest first, in one thread. Each conversation has an owner and a status,
and you answer from it in the channel the customer last used. The module
requires =crm= and =activities=; mail and telephony are channels it reads
when they are on.

Open *Conversations* in the sidebar, under Customers, or =/conversations=.
On a contact's page, *Conversation* opens theirs.

* What is in a conversation

A conversation shows what is already recorded about the contact. Nothing is
copied into the inbox: each message is read from its own record every time
the conversation is shown, so a message corrected or removed in its own
place is corrected or removed here too.

| Channel   | Where it comes from                                                                                          |
|-----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Email     | The contact's email history: mail the mailbox sync filed, and mail you sent once the mail relay accepted it ([[file:mail.org][Mail]]) |
| Text      | The contact's text messages, both ways ([[file:telephony.org][Phone calls and text messages]])                                  |
| Call      | Every logged call on the contact: answered, missed and voicemail ([[file:activities.org][Activities]], Logging calls)               |
| Voicemail | A call whose result is voicemail. Its recording link and transcript show only when the call holds them, which is only when your organization turned recording links on |
| Web chat  | Messages from the chat on your website (below)                                                                |

Each channel adds itself to the inbox from its own code; a new channel adds
one file and changes nothing else.

* Statuses and defaults

| Status  | Means                                   |
|---------+-----------------------------------------|
| Open    | It needs an answer from you             |
| Waiting | You answered; it waits on the customer  |
| Closed  | Nothing more to do                      |

- The first message from or to a contact starts their conversation, *open*
  and *unassigned*.
- Anything new *from the customer*, on any channel, reopens a waiting or
  closed conversation.
- Your own replies never change the status. Set it with the buttons on the
  conversation (*Waiting on them*, *Close*, *Reopen*).
- Mail and calls from before the inbox was switched on are already in the
  thread. The contact's conversation is made when they next write, or when
  someone opens it from their page.

* Owners

A conversation has at most one owner: a person who is an active member of
the organization (or runs the whole installation). Choose one on the
conversation, or *Assign to me*. Every change of owner and status is saved
in the audit log with who made it, and the conversation lists that history.

The list has three views: *Mine* (you own it), *Unassigned* and *All*,
each filtered by status (open by default) and by channel.

* Replying

The reply box answers in the channel of the customer's latest message:

- *Email* goes through the outbox as a threaded reply to their message, the
  same as replying from the contact's timeline. It appears in the
  conversation once the mail relay accepts it.
- *Text* goes through the one text send path, so text consent and quiet
  hours apply: without their agreement the reply is refused, and during
  their quiet hours it is held and sent when they end.
- *Web chat* appears on the visitor's chat page.
- After a *call*, the conversation offers *Call back* (through the phone
  system, ringing your own extension first) and the number itself. You may
  still write by choosing another channel under *Send by*.

* Web chat

An owner or administrator adds a chat under
=/organizations/ID/settings/webchat=. Each chat has a public address,
=https://your-venture/webchat/TOKEN=: link to it, or put it on your site in a
frame (the settings page shows the code). List the sites that may frame it,
or leave the list empty to allow any.

The visitor gives their name, an email address or a phone number, and a
message. Venture finds the contact with that email (compared the way lead
capture compares addresses), then that phone number, or makes a new contact
with source "Web chat". The visitor is sent to their own chat page,
=/webchat/c/SECRET=, which shows only that chat: their messages and your
replies, never anything else about the contact. The page works without
script; with script it checks for replies every ten seconds.

Limits:

- a name of up to 100 characters, an email of up to 254, a phone of up to 32,
  a message of up to 2,000 characters, a request of up to 16 KiB;
- one address may start 5 chats on one chat in 10 minutes, and one chat
  takes 20 messages in 10 minutes;
- a hidden field a person leaves empty: a form robot that fills it is
  thanked and nothing is kept.

The visitor's page needs no account and no cookie. Its address is the key:
a long random secret that Venture keeps only as a hash, so the address
cannot be recovered from the database. Anyone with the address can read
that chat, so it is the visitor's to keep. The pages send no referrer and
are not cached. A switched-off chat answers "not available" at once.

* What is not in it

- *Your notifications.* The sidebar's *Inbox* (=/inbox=) is your own
  notifications -- mentions, watched records, reminders. It is about you,
  not about a customer, and stays separate.
- *A second timeline.* The contact's page keeps its own timeline; the
  conversation reads the same records.
- *Notes, meetings and tasks.* Only what a customer said or was told.
- *Automatic replies and summaries.* Not yet.

* API

| Method | Path                                   | Role   | Does                                                          |
|--------+----------------------------------------+--------+---------------------------------------------------------------|
| GET    | =/api/v1/conversations=                | viewer | List: =view= (mine, unassigned, all), =status= (open, waiting, closed, any), =channel=, =organization_id= |
| GET    | =/api/v1/conversations/:id=            | viewer | The conversation, its =items= oldest first and the =reply_channel= |
| POST   | =/api/v1/conversations/:id/assign=     | editor | ={"owner_id": N}=, ="me"= or =0= to unassign                  |
| POST   | =/api/v1/conversations/:id/status=     | editor | ={"status": "waiting"}=                                       |
| POST   | =/api/v1/conversations/:id/reply=      | editor | ={"body": "...", "channel": "text"}=; =channel= is optional   |
| GET    | =/api/v1/contacts/:id/conversation=    | viewer | A contact's thread and conversation                           |

Each item names its =channel=, the =record_type= and =record_id= it is, when
it happened, whether it came from the customer and a one-line =summary=.
The records themselves are read through the ordinary record API.

* Upgrades and tests

Migration =000930_inbox_conversations_and_web_chat= checks the four new
tables; they start empty. =test-inbox= covers source registration, the
thread's order across channels and its cursor, that the inbox stores no
message words, defaults, assignment and its audit, statuses and reopening,
the reply channel, consent and quiet hours on a text reply, web chat
bounds, limits, matching and link secrecy, organization isolation and the
pages; =test-auth= the sign-in and role refusals of every route.
