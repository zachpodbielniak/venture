#+title: Leads

* Inquiries before opportunities

The =leads= module requires =crm=. Its =lead=, =lead_form= and
=lead_assignment_rule= records use the ordinary generated forms, REST,
CLI and import machinery. An inquiry has its own qualification state;
a deal's existing stage remains the opportunity's stage.

A lead belongs to one organization and may name a venture and a campaign.
Its source, score, owner, contact details and notes describe the inquiry.
Conversion references and timestamps preserve the eventual handoff.
