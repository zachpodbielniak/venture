#+title: Purchase orders, receiving and three-way match
#+description: Commit spend, receive stock and match the vendor bill before it can hit AP

* Purchase orders

The =goods= module requires =payables=, =ledger= and =sales=. It owns
=purchase_order=, =purchase_order_line=, =goods_receipt= and
=goods_receipt_line=. Field tables supply the generic forms, REST
resources and CLI. =VenturePurchasingService= owns every status
transition.

A draft names a vendor, a number unique inside the organization, a
currency and lines with whole-unit quantity and an exact unit price.
=approve= then =send= freeze the commercial commitment. =cancel= is
refused once quantity has been received; return the stock first.

* Receiving

=receive= posts a =goods_receipt= for a partial or complete quantity,
writes a signed =inventory_txn= and a FIFO cost layer, and journals
Inventory 1200 against Goods received not invoiced 2010 through
=VenturePostingService=. Quantity on hand remains the sum of
=inventory_txn= rows. Over-receipt is refused.

* Three-way match

=match= links a draft =vendor_bill= to the purchase order and compares
received value to billed value within =match-tolerance-percent=. A
mismatch stamps =match-status=mismatch= and bill approval is refused
until an exception is approved. A match (or an approved exception)
points the bill lines at the GRNI account so =VenturePayablesService=
still posts AP and does not invent a second payable journal.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl purchase approve 1
venturectl purchase send 1
venturectl purchase receive LINE quantity=4
venturectl purchase match 1 vendor_bill_id=2
venturectl bill approve 2
#+end_src
