#+title: Purchase orders, receiving and three-way match
#+description: Commit spend, receive stock and match the vendor bill before it can hit AP

* Purchase orders

A complete return restores the order to =sent=, permitting replacement receipts or cancellation once no received quantity remains.

Matching updates the order, bill lines and bill in one transaction. A failed account assignment or bill save rolls back every part of the match. Generic line edits require a draft parent and cannot move evidence out of an approved order.

The =goods= module requires =payables=, =ledger= and =sales=. It owns
=purchase_order=, =purchase_order_line=, =goods_receipt= and
=goods_receipt_line=. Field tables supply the generic forms, REST
resources and CLI. =VenturePurchasingService= owns every status
transition.

A draft names a vendor, a number unique inside the organization, a
currency and lines with whole-unit quantity and an exact unit price.
=approve= then =send= freeze the commercial commitment. =cancel= is
refused once quantity has been received; return the stock first.

* Receiving

=receive= posts a =goods_receipt= for a partial or complete quantity,
writes a signed =inventory_txn= and a FIFO cost layer, and journals
Inventory 1200 against Goods received not invoiced 2010 through
=VenturePostingService=. Quantity on hand remains the sum of
=inventory_txn= rows. Over-receipt is refused.

* Receiving in another currency

A purchase order is in one currency, and its lines must be in it (a save
validator refuses a line that is not). Receiving costs the stock in that
currency: the FIFO layer carries it, and the Inventory/GRNI pair posts
by the currency's book treatment (see [[file:currencies.org][Currencies]]) -- converted into the
book currency when a =valued= currency has a rate, a journal of its own
for =separate_book=, nothing at all for =memo=. Selling the unit later
posts the revenue in the sale's currency and the cost of goods in the
layer's, so a lantern bought for tickets and sold for gold is GOLD
revenue and TICKET cost (see [[file:inventory.org][Inventory]]).

The whole flow is reachable without the workbench: the REST route below
is what =venturectl purchase= drives, and it takes every value as the
CLI sends it, a string (="quantity": "2"=). =/inventory-currencies/http-ticket-purchase=
receives a TICKET order through it and sells the unit for gold.

A purchase order is not a payment. For a memo currency paid out of a
character's purse, record the spend as a =holding_txn= (=kind=spend=,
negative); for a posted currency the vendor bill carries it.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl create purchase_order number=FAIRE-1 vendor_id=3 currency=TICKET status=draft
venturectl create purchase_order_line purchase_order_id=7 inventory_item_id=9 \
    description=Lantern quantity=2 position=1 unit_price="4 TICKET"
venturectl purchase approve 7
venturectl purchase send 7
venturectl purchase receive 7 line_id=12 quantity=2
#+end_src

* Committed spend

The =committed_spend= report lists every approved, sent or partly
received order with what it still commits -- ordered less received, at
the line's price -- in the order's own currency. Totals are per
currency: =committed= in the organization's book currency (zero when it
has none), =committed_<CODE>= for each other, the rule every per-currency
report follows. It used to start from zero dollars, so any order in
another currency failed the whole report.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl report committed_spend all organization_id=2
#+end_src

* Three-way match

=match= links a draft =vendor_bill= to the purchase order and compares
received value to billed value within =match-tolerance-percent=. Bill
quantity is an exact decimal (up to three places); billing more than
was received is a mismatch even when the money is inside the price
tolerance. A mismatch stamps =match-status=mismatch= and bill approval
is refused until an exception is approved. A PO-linked bill that has
not been matched is also refused. A match (or an approved exception)
points the bill lines at the mapped GRNI control account (role
=grni=, else 2010) so =VenturePayablesService= still posts AP and does
not invent a second payable journal.

The =/purchasing= workbench posts =approve=, =send=, =receive= (line
and quantity) and =match= (bill id, optional exception).

#+begin_src sh
venturectl purchase approve 1
venturectl purchase send 1
venturectl purchase receive 1 line_id=LINE quantity=4
venturectl purchase match 1 vendor_bill_id=2
venturectl bill approve 2
#+end_src
