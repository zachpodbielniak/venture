#+title: Settings: marketing, sales, phone and AI
#+description: Every workflow setting -- what it does, its default and its limit -- and the four ways to change one: the settings screen, the assistant, the API and venturectl

How email, campaigns, sales, phone and text, web chat and AI on messages
behave is a short list of settings per organization. Each has a sensible
default, so a new organization needs none of them. This page lists every
one, and how to change it.

* Where they are

Open the organization's page and choose *Marketing, sales, phone and AI*,
or go to =/organizations/ID/settings/workflows=. Owners and administrators
of the organization see the screen; others are refused.

The screen groups the settings by workflow. Each shows what it does, what
it is now, its default and its limit. *Save* changes it, *Reset to default*
puts the default back. It works at phone width: each setting stacks above
its controls.

* What changing one does

- The value is checked first: on or off, a whole number inside its
  limits, one of its choices, or text in the form the setting describes.
  A refused value is answered in words and nothing is written.
- Only an owner or administrator of that organization may change it.
- The value is written where the feature has always kept it -- a field on
  the organization, the email brand, the first sending domain, an
  auto-reply rule, a web chat. Nothing is moved into a separate store, and
  the feature's own pages (the AI messages page, the web chat page, the
  deliverability page) keep working on the same values.
- The change is one entry in the audit log, saying who made it and the old
  and new value. Setting the value a setting already has writes nothing.
- A setting belongs to one organization; another organization keeps its own.
- When a setting's module is switched off, the setting is not offered.

* The settings

Defaults are what a new organization has. "Stored" is where the value lives.

** Email

| Setting | Key | Default | Limit | Stored |
|---------+-----+---------+-------+--------|
| Check links answer on every preview | =mail.preview-link-check= | off | on or off | organization |
| Brand colour (headings, buttons) | =email-brand.primary-color= | =#1f5fbf= | =#rrggbb= | email brand |
| Accent colour (links) | =email-brand.accent-color= | =#0f766e= | =#rrggbb= | email brand |
| Text colour | =email-brand.text-color= | =#1f2933= | =#rrggbb= | email brand |
| Background colour | =email-brand.background-color= | =#f3f4f6= | =#rrggbb= | email brand |
| Font | =email-brand.font= | sans | sans, serif, rounded, system, mono | email brand |

- *Check links answer on every preview*: each mail preview also asks
  every web link in the message whether it answers -- one request each,
  at most ten, from this server. Off, a preview checks only that links are
  well formed and offers the check as a link ([[file:mail.org][Mail]]). =?reachability=0= on a
  preview still skips it once.
- *The brand*: the look of designed email ([[file:email-design.org][Designed email]]). Changing a
  brand setting when the organization has no brand yet makes one, named
  after the organization. A designed template picks the change up when it
  is next saved, or at once for all with =rebuild_designs=.

** Campaigns

| Setting | Key | Default | Limit | Stored |
|---------+-----+---------+-------+--------|
| Warm-up daily limits | =marketing.warm-up= | =1:50,3:100,5:250,7:500,10:1000,14:2500,21:5000,30:0= | day:limit pairs from day 1, or =off= | first sending domain |
| Local-time hour for new sends | =marketing.local-hour= | 9 | 0 to 23 | organization |
| A/B test wait | =marketing.ab-wait-hours= | 4 hours | 1 to 168 | organization |
| A/B test winner by | =marketing.ab-metric= | opens | opens, clicks | organization |
| Resend to non-openers after | =marketing.resend-days= | 3 days | 0 to 365 | organization |

- *Warm-up*: how many campaign messages may go each day while a new
  sending domain builds trust ([[file:deliverability.org][Deliverability]], Warm-up). 0 is no limit;
  =off= turns the warm-up off. It lives on the organization's first sending
  domain, so add the domain on the Deliverability page first; until then
  the default applies and changing it is refused with that advice.
- *Local-time hour*: a new local-time send whose hour is left at 0 starts
  at this hour in each recipient's own time ([[file:marketing.org][Marketing]], Recipient local
  time). To send at midnight, set the send's hour to 0 after making it.
- *A/B test wait*: a send that sets no wait picks its winner this many
  hours after the test messages go.
- *A/B winner by*: a new send starts with this; change it on the send.
- *Resend to non-openers*: the days a resend waits when the action names none.

** Sales

| Setting | Key | Default | Limit | Stored |
|---------+-----+---------+-------+--------|
| AI lead score proposals | =leads.ai-scoring= | off | on or off | organization |

The assistant proposes a lead score with its reasons beside the rule
score; a person accepts or dismisses each ([[file:leads.org][Leads]], AI score proposals).
It needs the organization's AI.

** Phone and text

| Setting | Key | Default | Limit | Stored |
|---------+-----+---------+-------+--------|
| Text quiet hours | =telephony.quiet-hours= | =21:00-08:00= | =HH:MM-HH:MM= on the 24-hour clock, or =off= | organization |
| Keep call recording links | =telephony.recording-links= | off | on or off | organization |
| Phone country | =telephony.country= | US | a two-letter country Venture knows the calling code of | organization |
| Answer to STOP | =telephony.stop-reply= | "{business}: you are unsubscribed and will get no more texts from us. Reply START to resubscribe." | one line, 320 characters, must say START | organization |
| Answer to START | =telephony.start-reply= | "{business}: you will get our texts again. Reply STOP to opt out." | one line, 320 characters, must say STOP | organization |
| Answer to HELP | =telephony.help-reply= | "{business}: reply STOP to stop our texts or START to get them again. Message and data rates may apply." | one line, 320 characters, must say STOP | organization |

- *Quiet hours* are in the recipient's own time zone, enforced in the one
  text send path ([[file:telephony.org][Phone calls and text messages]], Quiet hours).
- *Recording links*: recording calls has legal rules where you and your
  callers are; read the recording note before turning this on.
- *Keyword answers*: ={business}= becomes the organization's name. The
  answers must keep telling people how to stop or come back; the keywords
  themselves (STOP, START, HELP and their synonyms) are fixed.

** Conversations

| Setting | Key | Default | Limit | Stored |
|---------+-----+---------+-------+--------|
| Web chat on (each chat) | =inbox.webchat.ID= | on | on or off | that web chat |

One setting per web chat on your site ([[file:inbox.org][Conversations]], Web chat). Off, the chat
takes no new messages; conversations already started stay in the inbox.

** AI on messages

| Setting | Key | Default | Limit | Stored |
|---------+-----+---------+-------+--------|
| Switch AI messages off | =ai-messages.off= | off (AI messages on) | on or off | organization |
| Business hours | =ai-messages.business-hours= | none | for example =Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00; Sat 10:00-14:00= | organization |
| Send without asking (each rule) | =ai-messages.auto-send.ID= | off | on or off | that auto-reply rule |

- *The switch*: while it is on, nothing is read, drafted, summarised or
  sent by the AI ([[file:ai-messages.org][AI on messages]]).
- *Business hours*: in the organization's time zone; after-hours rules
  answer outside them. None means no after-hours replies.
- *Send without asking*: one setting per auto-reply rule. Off, the rule's
  reply waits as a draft for a person to approve.

* How to change one

** On the screen

=/organizations/ID/settings/workflows=: change the value, *Save*; or *Reset
to default*.

** In chat

Ask the assistant, for example "make the text quiet hours 8pm to 7am". It
has three tools: =settings_list= (every setting, or one workflow's),
=settings_get= (one by key) and =settings_set= (a value, or =reset=).
=settings_set= never changes anything by itself, under any policy: it
stages the change and the panel shows the card with *Apply* and *Discard*.
Applying needs an owner or administrator of the organization; an editor's
approval is refused. The applied change is the same record change, and
the same single audit entry, as saving it on the screen -- the entry
differs only in who: the actor is the assistant, with the prompt, the
confirmation's id and the person who approved it.

** Over the API

#+begin_src sh
# every setting of an organization (organization_id is optional: the default one)
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $VENTURE_TOKEN" \
     "$VENTURE_SERVER/api/v1/workflow-settings?organization_id=1"

# one
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $VENTURE_TOKEN" \
     "$VENTURE_SERVER/api/v1/workflow-settings/telephony.quiet-hours"

# change one, or put it back
curl -X PUT -H "Authorization: Bearer $VENTURE_TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
     -d '{"value": "20:00-07:00"}' \
     "$VENTURE_SERVER/api/v1/workflow-settings/telephony.quiet-hours"
curl -X PUT -H "Authorization: Bearer $VENTURE_TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
     -d '{"reset": true}' \
     "$VENTURE_SERVER/api/v1/workflow-settings/telephony.quiet-hours"
#+end_src

Each setting comes back as =key=, =workflow=, =label=, =help=, =type=
(boolean, integer, text or choice), =value=, =default=, =is_default=,
=minimum= and =maximum= or =choices=, =permission= and =stored_in=. A
switch takes =true= or =false=, a number a number; text works for any.
Anybody in the organization may read; changing needs an owner or
administrator (403). A refused value is 422 with the reason; an unknown
key, or one whose module is off, is 404. (=/api/v1/settings= is
something else: the install's configuration, owner only.)

** With venturectl

#+begin_src sh
venturectl settings list [organization_id=N]
venturectl settings get telephony.quiet-hours
venturectl settings set telephony.quiet-hours 20:00-07:00
venturectl settings reset telephony.quiet-hours
#+end_src

=venturectl= calls the API above, with its token.

* For developers

A feature registers its settings from its own file with
=venture_settings_register()= (=src/settings/venture-settings.h=): key,
workflow, module, label, help, type, default, bounds or choices,
permission, where it is stored, the field, what the field holds when
nothing is set, and -- when the record is not the organization -- a
function that finds it. A family (one setting per rule or per chat) adds a
function listing its members. The screen, the API, venturectl and the
assistant pick a new registration up with nothing else written. The
registrations live in =src/mail/venture-mail-preview-settings.c=,
=src/emaildesign/venture-email-design-settings.c=,
=src/marketing/venture-marketing-settings.c=,
=src/leads/venture-lead-ai-score.c=,
=src/telephony/venture-telephony-settings.c=,
=src/inbox/venture-inbox-settings.c= and
=src/aimessages/venture-ai-messages-settings.c=.

Migration =000955_settings_workflow_defaults= checks the organization
fields added for defaults that had no home (link checks, the send
defaults, the keyword answers). They arrive empty, which reads as the
defaults above; nothing already stored moves. =tests/test-venture-settings.c=
covers the registrations, defaults, validation and limits, permission,
the single audit entry, reset, families, storage, the features reading
the values, organization isolation and the four ways in reaching the same
audit entry.
