#+title: Financial statements
#+description: Posted ledger statements, comparisons and source reconciliation

* Authority and scope

The immutable accounting authority is the posted ledger (accrual recognition).
Reports accept =basis= of =accrual= (default) or =cash=. Cash recognition uses
receipts and payments: invoice issues and bill approvals are excluded, and
allocated receipts recognize frozen net income and tax in the receipt period.
Cash also reclassifies AR and AP by the same receipt or payment so the
balance sheet and cash-flow statement still tie, including later periods
where the receipt is opening cash. Bill cash uses the paid bill's expense
account rather than assuming code 6900. Compare-to periods use the
same basis. The statements page keeps a Basis selector on every report form.
Exports, dashboards and period snapshots retain the selected basis in their
options; snapshots default to accrual so closed history is the ledger.



The =statements= module requires =ledger= and =periods=. It registers
=balance_sheet=, =income_statement=, =cash_flow=, =general_ledger=,
=account_balances= and =pnl_reconciliation=. No new persistent records are
introduced. =VentureLedgerBalances= is the reusable query object; its
construct-only =database= property selects the repository.

All amounts come from journal lines' book amounts. Drafts, standalone legacy
ledger entries and mutable =account.opening_balance= fields do not contribute.
A reversed original still contributes at its original accounting date; its
reversal contributes on its own date. Opening evidence must be posted as a
balanced journal. No posting, journal or trial-balance behavior is changed.

=organization_id= selects exactly one legal entity, defaulting to the context's
default organization. Selecting a parent company does not consolidate its
children. =currency= selects one uppercase book currency. Without it, each
currency has separate rows and totals; no exchange conversion or cross-currency
netting occurs. An empty ledger reports zero in the configured default currency.

* Dates and comparisons

The existing period parser selects an inclusive start and exclusive end in
the configured reporting timezone. =as_of= optionally caps the accounting
cutoff: an ISO date includes that UTC day, and an ISO timestamp includes that
instant. Entries after the earlier of that cutoff and period end are excluded.
As-of dates do not undo subsequent edits to account descriptions or hierarchy.

Every report accepts =compare_to= with a prior period label. It must end at or
before the current period starts. =prior= and =delta= compare the row's primary
amount: the statement value, account closing balance, reconciliation difference,
or general-ledger running balance. The prior period uses its own endpoint;
the current as-of cutoff does not truncate that prior period.

General-ledger rows compare each running balance with that account's closing
balance in the prior period. They do not pretend that unrelated journals in
two periods are matching transactions. IDs are decimal strings, preserving
all integer digits in JSON and CSV.

* Definitions

** Balance sheet

Asset, liability and equity rows show closing balances on their normal side:
assets are debits minus credits; liabilities and equity are credits minus
debits. Each account row includes descendants. Section totals count direct
account balances once, so parent subtotals are never added again.

=retained_income= is income less expenses before the selected period that
remains in income/expense accounts. =net_income= is income less expenses during
the selected period. Both are included in total equity alongside posted equity
balances; an explicit closing transfer is therefore not counted twice.
=assets=, =liabilities= and =equity= are section totals. =difference= is assets
less liabilities and equity, required to equal zero exactly.

** Income statement and reconciliation

Income is credits minus debits in income accounts; expenses are debits minus
credits in expense accounts, during the selected period. =net_income= is income
less expenses. This is recognized ledger activity, independent of payment or
operational document status. Parent account rows roll up; totals do not repeat
the rollups.

A note directs readers to =pnl_reconciliation= when sources disagree with the
operational P&L. That supporting report groups posted profit contributions by
source type, source ID and currency, then compares them with the source's sale
net or full expense amount in the same range. Only this reconciliation reads
sales/expenses; they never supply statement totals. Deleted documents, invoice
accruals, receipts represented by cash-report sales and manual journals can
explain differences. Positive =difference= means ledger profit is greater.
=ledger= and =operational= show both contributions; =current= repeats the
difference for comparisons. Zero-difference documents are omitted.

** Cash flow, indirect

Start with ledger net income. Add credits minus debits in every non-cash
balance-sheet account: increasing receivables or inventory reduces cash;
increasing payables or tax payable increases it. Account rows expose each
adjustment. The four control subtotals use conventional chart codes and their
descendants: receivables =1100=, payables =2000=, inventory =1200= and tax payable
=2100=. Cash is =1000= and its descendants. Organization-prefixed conventional
codes created by the posting rules are also recognized.

=adjustments= totals all non-cash balance-sheet movements, including the four
control groups and other asset, liability and equity accounts. The latter
include investing, financing and non-cash offsets; this statement does not
classify a full statutory operating/investing/financing presentation.
Control subtotals summarize the account rows and must not be added a second
time. There is no unexplained residual or balancing plug.

=cash_start= and =cash_end= are Cash balances at the range boundaries.
=cash_movement= is net income plus adjustments. =difference= compares this
with Cash closing less Cash opening and must be zero exactly. Non-cash offsets
cancel through the same ledger identity. No FX cash-flow adjustment or
cash-equivalent classification is inferred. A custom chart must retain the
conventional control codes or provide a replacement report through the registry.

** Account balances and general ledger

=account_balances= lists every account with =opening=, unsigned =debits=,
unsigned =credits= and =closing=. Opening and closing use debit minus credit.
The opening is posted evidence before the start; closing is opening plus
debits less credits. Parent rows include descendants. The query API's =rollup=
argument can instead select direct account amounts. Cyclic or cross-organization
parent hierarchies are refused rather than silently changing totals.

=general_ledger= lists every posted line in the range, optionally narrowed
by =account_id=. Within each account and currency, order is accounting date,
journal ID and line ID. =current= is the running debit-minus-credit balance
including opening evidence. Debit, credit, accounting date, journal ID and
source type/ID accompany each line.

* Surfaces and preserved evidence

The Statements section on =/reports= uses the registered reports and existing
report pages. Period, as-of and comparison controls preserve the report options
in links and CSV exports. Actual account rows carry =account_id=; general-ledger
rows also carry =journal_id=. The shared HTML renderer links these IDs to their
record pages, including =/e/journal/ID=. Synthetic totals have an empty account
ID because no single account owns them.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl report balance_sheet 2026-08 organization_id=1 currency=USD
venturectl report income_statement 2026-08 organization_id=1 currency=USD compare_to=2026-07
venturectl -f csv report general_ledger 2026-08 organization_id=1 currency=USD account_id=1
#+end_src

All six reports are financial and participate in the existing close transaction.
Period close saves their exact results in =report_snapshot=; =snapshot_vs_live=
compares them through the registry. A later open-period reversal changes that
later period, retaining the closed statement and its original posted evidence.
Reopening and editing account descriptions can still produce report drift;
a snapshot is preserved evidence, not a versioned chart of accounts.

* Verification

=test-statements= covers balances, parent rollups, two currencies, exact legal
entity scope, statement totals, comparative columns, month-end cutoffs,
reconciliation, running balances, HTML journal links, the real report pages,
the CLI CSV subprocess, module disablement, fiscal snapshots and later
