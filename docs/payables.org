#+title: Vendor payables
#+description: Supplier bills, payments and dated liability evidence

* The supplier's bill

The =payables= module requires =finance=, =ledger= and =crm=. The last
requirement supplies the supplier company; switching the module off hides
its records and reports together. Bills belong to one organization and
numbering is unique inside that organization.

=vendor_bill= holds the supplier, number, bill and due dates, currency,
status and memo. =vendor_bill_line= holds description, exact decimal
quantity, unit price, expense account or category, and tax amount.
Quantity is a decimal string with at most three fractional digits, parsed
into an integer rational. Price multiplication rounds once, half to even;
tax is then added in the same currency. No floating-point arithmetic is
used. Tax is part of the expense cost; recoverable input-tax accounting is
outside this module.

* One financial writer

=VenturePayablesService= belongs to the database and owns approval,
allocations, vendor credits, refunds and voiding. Generic status changes
are refused with the service named. Approval freezes the amount and due
date in =vendor_bill_event= and posts each line's expense account against
Accounts payable 2000. Approved lines, currency and dates cannot be edited.
An unpaid approved bill can be voided; its approval is reversed with a new
dated posting. Historical evidence cannot be removed through deletion,
restoration or purge.

=bill_payment= records money paid to a supplier. =bill_payment_allocation=
applies exactly one payment or vendor credit to a bill. A payment can cover
several bills in one transaction through the service's allocation-array
API. A single-bill payment applies up to that bill's remaining amount;
the excess is a =vendor_credit=. Remaining credit and bill status are
derived from retained allocations and refunds. A =bill_refund= records
cash returned by the supplier and reopens an allocated bill when applicable.
Payments debit AP and credit Cash 1000. Vendor credit notes debit AP and
credit General expenses 6900. Refunds reverse the cash movement.

Every financial operation shares one transaction with its audit entries,
allocations and journals. A failure after writing the payment leaves no
payment or partial journal. Dated events check the fiscal-period guard
inside that transaction; updating a derived current balance does not
rewrite its earlier accounting date. Currency differences are refused.

Account IDs on lines select active expense accounts within the bill's
organization. Service properties select the cash, payable and default
expense accounts. Zero selects conventional codes 1000, 2000 and 6900;
missing default accounts are created within the transaction.

* Scope

Bank matching and purchase orders/receiving are separate modules. No
historical approvals are inferred from an imported bill's current status.
