Organization SMTP isolation demonstration

Built source: 4dd239d (same tested source immediately before commit).
Command: make DEBUG=1 test-mail-relay
Dependencies: mail-glib 54fc361; isolated Mailpit v1.29.2 with required
STARTTLS, authenticated synthetic accounts and a temporary trusted CA.
Only loopback traffic and synthetic recipients are used.

The executable configures A and B through VentureIntegrationService,
sends once from each through VentureMailOutbox, rotates only A to an
invalid password, and sends once more from each. Expected visible states:
A sent, B sent, A dead (authentication refused), B sent. The same executable
asserts the relay retained exactly three messages, one from A and two from
B, and that connection status excludes all credential values. "Sent" means
relay acceptance; this does not demonstrate delivery to an external inbox.
