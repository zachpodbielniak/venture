* Imported records and revocation

The GTest transport fixtures separately run actual import services, persistence
and access checks. Private mail does not create CRM interactions. Private
calendar events use typed record fields, do not create/export shared
activities, and retain their identity after remote removal and restoration.
The inflight-revocation tests disconnect a binding from a fake transport's
=response-ready= callback; the completed response must not be imported.
These are deterministic synthetic provider demonstrations, not evidence of
production IMAP/CalDAV interoperability or hosted-tenant recovery.
