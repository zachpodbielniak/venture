#+title: Forms accessibility evidence
#+description: What checks the forms renderer's accessibility, and what the checks reported
#+created: [2026-09-28]

* What is checked, and by what

- =/forms/a11y-contract= (=tests/test-forms.c=, in =make test=): a
  structural checker over libxml2's parse of every field kind (all twelve,
  alternating required, some with help), in five renderings: the bare
  fragment, the hosted page, the hosted page with every question refused
  and one unknown name, and the thank-you bare and as a page. It fails on
  an unnamed control, a duplicate id, an =aria-describedby= naming nothing,
  a =fieldset= without a first =legend=, a required control that does not
  say so to assistive technology (or a radio group that does not), an
  =aria-invalid= without a visible message tied to it, refused answers
  with no visible focusable summary, a summary link to nowhere, a positive
  =tabindex=, a =style= attribute, an empty button, required questions
  with no note, and a page with no =lang= or =<title>=.
- =/forms/a11y-checker-catches=: ten pieces of bad markup, one per rule;
  the checker must fail each.
- The contract test fails against the renderer as it was before the
  accessibility child: radio groups did not carry =aria-required= and
  nothing said what the asterisk meant.
- =tools/venture-forms-axe.sh=: axe-core 4 in jsdom over the same five
  renderings, colour contrast excluded (no layout in jsdom; the host
  page's colours are the host's).

* axe-core run

Against the epic branch after the accessibility child:

#+begin_example
$ tools/venture-forms-axe.sh --keep /tmp/forms-a11y-out
==> rendering into /tmp/forms-a11y-out
ok bare-thanks.html: 0 violation(s), 12 rule(s) passed
ok blank.html: 0 violation(s), 27 rule(s) passed
ok page.html: 0 violation(s), 29 rule(s) passed
ok refused.html: 0 violation(s), 34 rule(s) passed
ok thanks.html: 0 violation(s), 14 rule(s) passed
#+end_example

* What is not claimed here

No human screen-reader session and no cross-browser certification. The
keyboard-only walkthrough of a multi-page form with an error is recorded
when multi-page forms land.
