#+title: Contractor 1099-NEC
#+description: Protected TIN records and calendar-year 1099-NEC packs from paid bills

* TIN is stored, not shown

=contractor_tax_form= holds a vendor TIN. The column is
=VENTURE_COLUMN_FLAG_SENSITIVE=: it is omitted from JSON, YAML, generic CSV
export, forms and the AI. Error messages never repeat it. Organization
finance access applies because =tax_filing= requires =finance=, the same
gate payroll would use.

* Prepare from paid bills

=venture_tax_filing_service_prepare_1099()= totals =bill_payment= rows for
one vendor in a calendar year. The IRS 1099-NEC threshold is 600 USD;
below that the prepare is refused. A contractor tax form must already
exist. A second prepare for the same vendor and year returns the existing
pack.

Review, then approve, then export. Approve freezes the 1099-NEC CSV
(including TIN) on the pack. Export after that returns those exact bytes;
a second export of the same year and vendor is identical. Export before
approval is refused.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl contractor-tax prepare vendor_id=1 year=2026
venturectl contractor-tax review 1
venturectl contractor-tax approve 1
venturectl contractor-tax export 1
#+end_src

The same actions sit on =/e/contractor_tax_pack=, and the prepare form
lives on =/tax-filings=.
