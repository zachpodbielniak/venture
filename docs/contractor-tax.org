#+title: Contractor 1099-NEC
#+description: Protected TIN records and calendar-year 1099-NEC packs from paid bills

* TIN is stored, not shown

=contractor_tax_form= holds a vendor TIN. The column is
=VENTURE_COLUMN_FLAG_SENSITIVE=: it is omitted from JSON, YAML, generic CSV
export, forms and the AI. Error messages never repeat it. Organization
finance access applies because =tax_filing= requires =finance=, the same
gate payroll would use.

* Prepare from paid bills

A failed export releases its transaction and operation guard, so a stale-version conflict does not block subsequent tax work. Re-read the pack before retrying a conflicted export.

=venture_tax_filing_service_prepare_1099()= totals =bill_payment= rows for
one vendor in a calendar year. The general threshold used by this bookkeeping pack is 600 USD for 2020–2025 and 2,000 USD for 2026; below that prepare is refused. Later years are refused until their inflation-adjusted threshold is implemented. A contractor tax form must already
exist. A second prepare for the same vendor and year returns the existing
pack.

Review, then approve, then export. Approve freezes the 1099-NEC CSV
(including TIN) on the pack. Export after that returns those exact bytes;
a second export of the same year and vendor is identical. Export before
approval is refused.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl contractor-tax prepare vendor_id=1 year=2026
venturectl contractor-tax review 1
venturectl contractor-tax approve 1
venturectl contractor-tax export 1
#+end_src

The same actions sit on =/e/contractor_tax_pack=, and the prepare form
lives on =/tax-filings=.

* Scope and source

This CSV is a bookkeeping review pack, not an IRS e-filing format or a determination of filing obligations. Totals currently include the vendor's recorded bill payments; an operator must review reportability, payment-method exclusions and backup-withholding exceptions separately. Currency must be USD. CSV fields quote commas, quotation marks and line breaks so names preserve their original content.

Threshold verified on 2026-09-15 against [[https://www.irs.gov/faqs/small-business-self-employed-other-business/form-1099-nec-and-independent-contractors][IRS Form 1099-NEC and independent contractors]] and [[https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099mec][IRS instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC]], which describe inflation adjustments beginning in 2027.
