#+title: Inventory to the general ledger
#+description: FIFO cost layers, COGS on issue, and a valuation that ties to account 1200

* Quantity and cost

Quantity on hand is still the sum of signed =inventory_txn= rows, never
a stored field. FIFO cost lives on =inventory_cost_layer=, written by
=VentureInventoryService= when goods are received. Issue consumes the
oldest remaining quantity and posts Cost of goods sold 5000 against
Inventory 1200 through =VenturePostingService=. The inventory control
account is resolved with setup role =inventory=, or code 1200.

=allow-negative= on =inventory_item= is false by default; a movement
that would take stock below zero is refused. Transfers move layers
between two items of the same product (two locations) without hitting
COGS.

* Production

A craft (see [[file:production.org][Production]]) turns stock into other
stock through =venture_inventory_service_produce()=, in one transaction:
each input leaves as a negative =production= transaction, consuming its
FIFO layers; the output arrives as a positive =production= transaction and
receives cost layers that carry exactly what was consumed -- split into at
most two layers (=r= units at one minor unit more, the rest at the floor)
so they sum to the consumed cost to the minor unit. No journal is posted:
the value moved from inventory to inventory, so account 1200 and
=inventory_valuation= are unchanged by a craft, and cost of goods sold is
booked when the made units are sold.

Units with no cost layer (an adjustment typed in by hand, or stock taken
below zero on an item that allows it) are consumed at no cost. Inputs whose
costs are in two currencies are refused. Without the =goods= module there
are no layers and a craft moves quantities only.

* Session yields

Posting a session (see [[file:sessions.org][Sessions]]) calls the same
=venture_inventory_service_produce()= with *no draws*: each goods yield
arrives as one positive =production= transaction, referenced
=session:<id>= and dated the session's end, and -- because nothing was
consumed -- receives one *zero-cost* layer, in the item's unit-cost
currency or else the organization's book currency. The yield's
=unit_value= is a valuation and is never written into a layer: a layer at
market value would book the profit when the goods were gathered and none
when they sold. The zero layer still matters, because the issue path
refuses units that have none. No journal is posted, so account 1200 does
not move, and =inventory_valuation= counts the units at zero.

Posting is idempotent: a yield stamped with its transaction is never
posted again, and a posted yield (and its session) cannot be deleted --
correct its stock with an adjustment instead.

* Where stock is

=inventory_item.location_id= names a =location= record: a place that can
nest inside another (a bin in a warehouse, a bag on a character) and be
renamed once for every item in it. The older free-text =location= column is
kept, labelled "Location (text)"; upgrading made one location per distinct
value and linked each item to it (migration =000695=). Items also carry
comma-separated, searchable =tags=. See [[file:taxonomy.org][Taxonomy]].

* Currencies

Receipt, issue and return journals go through
=venture_posting_service_post_by_currency()=, so a cost in a currency other
than the book currency follows the ledger's rule (see [[file:ledger.org][the
general journal]], "Currencies in the books"): converted into the book
journal when the currency is valued and has a rate, a pair of its own when
it is a separate book or has no rate, nothing when it is memo. That call
takes lines in several currencies at once and returns one journal per book,
each balanced by itself, which is the hook for an issue whose FIFO layers
were bought in two currencies. Layers themselves still carry one currency
each, and consuming a mixture in one issue is still refused; that is later
work.

* Autojournal

When a product has an =inventory_item=, automatic sale journals skip
the product-cost COGS legs. For a product without one, the COGS pair is in
the currency of the product's recorded =cost=, which need not be the
sale's: a gold sale of an item that cost tickets posts gold cash and sales
and ticket cost of goods sold, each by its currency's treatment. The inventory service owns those journals
so a sale cannot double-post inventory and COGS.

* Reports

=inventory_valuation= as-of is remaining FIFO layers. It is the figure
that must reconcile to the GL inventory account. =reorder_worklist=
lists items at or below their reorder point.
