#+title: Sales territories and quota attainment
#+description: Deterministic ownership and immutable booked-sales credit

* Enable and configure

The =sales_performance= module requires =orgaccess=, =leads= and
=pipelines=. Its four record types use the ordinary record editor, generic
API, CLI, audit, reference pickers and related-record sections:

| Record | Purpose |
|--------+---------|
| =sales_territory= | Active organization-owned territory and owning team |
| =sales_quota= | One representative or team target for one currency and period |
| =sales_assignment= | Immutable observed lead/deal ownership transition |
| =sales_credit= | Immutable booking or cancellation reversal |

Create an active team, its organization members and active team memberships
first. Create a territory pointing to that team. On an existing
=lead_routing_rule= choose the territory and the same team; existing rule
conditions, actions and ordering remain the assignment engine. Inspect
=venturectl describe sales_territory=, =venturectl describe sales_quota= and
=venturectl describe lead_routing_rule= for current wire field names and
reference targets. No external routing service is required.

Territory names and descriptions describe the business partition. Matching
conditions belong to routing rules, allowing several rules to use a
territory without introducing a second condition language. Territories may
overlap: the existing ascending rule =position=, then ID, determines which
matching eligible rule wins. Round robin uses the existing durable cursor;
only active users with active organization and team memberships participate.
An inactive territory is skipped. A rule whose territory and team no longer
agree fails clearly; update its team after moving a territory to a new team.
Changing a territory does not reassign existing leads or deals.

A lead's =owner_user_id=, =team_id= and =territory_id= accompany its existing
=owner= username. Routing and explicit rerouting fill these fields together.
Lead conversion carries them to the resulting deal. Quotas and credit snapshots
also use the canonical =owner_user_id= field so the existing member/owner/team
access policy applies; historical evidence remains immutable. Manual ownership changes
validate same-organization references and active membership; contradictory
username/user ID or team/territory combinations are refused. An existing
unchanged assignment remains editable after a representative is deactivated:
the deal still moves to a won stage and the booking is credited to that
representative, and converting a lead carries a routed territory or owner
onto the deal even after either has since been deactivated. Only choosing a
different assignment is judged against current activity.
Legacy free-form usernames with no resolved account remain unassigned or
unresolved for representative attainment; they do not receive guessed credit.

* Targets and periods

Set =metric= to =booked_revenue=, a positive exact-money =target=, and exactly
one of =owner_user_id= or =team_id=. The target's currency is mandatory. Set
=starts_at= to inclusive midnight UTC and =ends_at= to exclusive midnight UTC.
For January, use 1 January through 1 February. These are calendar boundaries,
not local-time instants. An end at or before the start is refused.

Monthly and quarterly quotas may overlap as independent rows. An exact
repeat of recipient, metric, currency and complete period is refused by a
derived durable unique key. Renaming or changing the target on that same
record is allowed. Do not add representative and team targets, or overlapping
period targets, together: they are different views of the same business.
Inactive representatives can retain targets and historical attainment.

* Booking credit and corrections

=booked_revenue= means the value captured when an observed deal becomes won.
It is a sales metric, separate from posted ledger revenue, invoices, receipts,
profitability and cash. The source deal's exact amount, currency and exponent,
representative, team and territory are copied into immutable evidence in the
same transaction as the transition. A missing value is an unvalued booking;
no currency or zero amount is invented. Negative booking values are refused.

Changing the current owner after a win creates assignment history but does
not move the original booking credit. Reopening or losing a won deal appends
a negative reversal at the cancellation time, credited to the original
representative and team. Winning it again creates a new booking for the then
current ownership. A later-period cancellation reduces that later period;
it does not rewrite a closed earlier period. A reopened legacy winner with
no captured booking creates no fabricated reversal.

Reopen a won deal before changing its value or currency, or before deleting
it. Derived credit and assignment records cannot be created, edited, deleted
or purged through generic writes. A source with sales evidence cannot be
purged; retain its history and archive it. A failed evidence write rolls back
the source transition as well. Unique event identities prevent repeated
writes of the same source revision from duplicating credit.

* Read the report

The generic =sales_attainment= report presents these columns together:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------+---------|
| Target | Exact target for this recipient, currency and full quota period |
| Actual | Signed captured bookings inside that full half-open period |
| Remaining | Maximum of target minus actual and zero |
| Attainment | Actual divided by target, displayed as a ratio |
| Open weighted | Current open pipeline value weighted by its probability |
| Committed | Current open value explicitly committed or at least 90% probable |
| Bookings | Signed total booking/reversal count |
| Unvalued bookings | Signed count without an amount or currency |

The requested report period selects intersecting quota windows. A selected
quota always reports its complete target window; requesting a partial month
does not compare a partial actual against a full target silently. Forecast
columns are the current open pipeline, following the existing forecast's
semantics, rather than a historical snapshot or a promised close-date filter.
Overlapping windows therefore repeat this current forecast independently.

Credits within the requested period without a matching quota appear as
=No quota=, grouped by recipient and currency. Targets and attainment are
absent there. Unvalued and unresolved assignments are explicit. Currencies
never combine or convert; representative and team rows remain separate.
Money additions, subtractions and weighting use VentureMoney integer
arithmetic. Only the display ratio uses a floating-point value.

The =uncaptured_wins= metric counts current winners with no captured booking.
On upgrade, existing winners are not backfilled: current ownership and value
cannot establish the facts at the historical win. Describe or correct those
records normally; only a later observed won transition creates new credit.

* Lifecycle and extension points

Disabling the module hides its records and report. Once its tables exist,
source transitions continue recording evidence, including reversals, so a
later enable does not resurrect cancelled attainment. An installation where
the module has never created tables continues ordinary lead/deal work; later
enabling it exposes historical winners as uncaptured. Migration 000580 checks
the metadata-created schema without inventing historical evidence.

The database save hook runs after the existing lead and pipeline authorization
hooks and wraps their continuation in the same transaction. Service-only
writes use an exact-object permit, and internal reads are limited to verified
organization membership or historical source evidence. Reports use the caller's
organization/access scope. Snapshot IDs deliberately are not live references:
removing a representative cannot prevent reversal of their earlier credit.

Extend supported metrics in the central quota validator, evidence producer
and report together; unknown metrics currently fail instead of silently
sharing booked revenue. Territory rules reuse the existing routing engine,
provider-independent records and durable round-robin state. No per-record
forms, routes, serialization or second routing implementation are added.

=tests/test-sales-performance.c= exercises routing overlap and inactive
eligibility; retained ownership and cancellation/rebooking credit; exact
currency-separated targets and weighted forecast; duplicate and invalid
quotas; exact period boundaries; transactional rollback; foreign references
and forged evidence; member-scoped routing and reporting; module disable/re-enable; and a populated pre-feature
upgrade without invented credit. The same fixture supports SQLite and an
explicit =VENTURE_TEST_ACCOUNTING_POSTGRES_URI=, creating and removing its own
unique PostgreSQL schema. Never point tests at an operational database.
