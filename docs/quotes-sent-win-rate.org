#+title: Quote sent win rate

* Quote sent win rate

=GET /api/v1/reports/quotes= returns =sent_win_rate= in its metrics array.
The report selects quotes by issue date, organization, and currency.
It counts current sent and accepted quotes in that cohort.
It excludes drafts, superseded revisions, and other currencies.

The metric divides the smaller of =accepted_count= and =sent_count= by =sent_count=.
This limit keeps the ratio at or below one.
The metric returns zero when =sent_count= is zero.

The report also returns =acceptance_rate=.
That metric divides accepted quotes by sent, accepted, declined, and expired quotes.
Do not use =acceptance_rate= as the sent win rate.
