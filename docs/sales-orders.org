#+title: Sales orders and fulfillment
#+description: Allocate stock, ship partial quantities and invoice only what has been fulfilled

* Sales orders

The =goods= module owns =sales_order=, =sales_order_line= and
=fulfillment=. A draft names a customer and lines. Stocked lines name
an =inventory_item=. =is-service= marks a line that may be invoiced
without a shipment.

=allocate= checks on-hand quantity. =ship= writes a =fulfillment=,
consumes FIFO and posts COGS. =invoice= / =invoice_fulfilled= compose
an invoice through =VentureDocumentService= and issue it through
=VentureSettlementService=. Stocked lines cannot be invoiced for more
than has been fulfilled. Service lines may invoice their ordered
quantity immediately.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl sales-order allocate 1
venturectl sales-order ship LINE quantity=2
venturectl sales-order invoice 1
#+end_src
