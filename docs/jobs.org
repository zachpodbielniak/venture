* What a job is

A home-services business works at its customers' addresses: a water heater
replaced, a furnace serviced, a drain cleared. A =job= is one such piece of
work. Before it existed the nearest thing was an activity of kind
"meeting", which has no customer address, no technicians, no promised
window and no link to the estimate or the bill.

The =jobs= module requires =crm= and suggests =quotes= and =invoicing=. It
owns two record types, both derived from their field tables like every
other: a list, a form, REST, CLI, JSON and the assistant's tools.

| Record | What it holds |
|--------+---------------|
| =job= | title, number, status, customer (=company_id=), contact on site, service address, problem, arrival window (=window_start=, =window_end=), estimate (=quote_id=), invoice (=invoice_id=), the times it was set off for, arrived at and finished, notes |
| =job_technician= | a job and a user sent to it; =lead= marks the one the customer deals with |

Jobs are under Customers in the sidebar, after Contacts.

* What the save holds to

- A job saved without a number takes the next free =J-= number in its
  business; deleted jobs count, so a number is never reused. A typed
  number is kept, trimmed, and is unique within the business.
- The customer is required and must be in the job's business. A contact on
  site must be too, and must not work for another customer.
- An arrival window ends after it starts. An end with no start is refused;
  a start alone is allowed ("from 8").
- The estimate and the invoice must be the job's customer's own. With
  =quotes= or =invoicing= switched off, naming one is refused with the
  switch named.
- An =invoiced= job names its invoice.
- A technician is an active user who can sign in, on a job in the same
  business, once per job.

* Through the day

The job's page offers only the buttons that apply to where it is. Each is
an ordinary record action (=POST /api/v1/job/:id/actions/<name>=,
=venturectl act job ID <name>=), needs an editor, and stamps when it
happened:

| Button | Action | From | To | Stamps |
|--------+--------+------+----+--------|
| On my way | =on_the_way= | booked | on the way | =left_at= |
| Arrived | =arrive= | booked, on the way | on site | =arrived_at= |
| Done | =complete= | booked, on the way, on site | done | =completed_at= |
| Invoiced | =invoiced= | done | invoiced | |
| Cancel job | =cancel= | booked, on the way, on site | cancelled | |

=invoiced= takes an optional =invoice_id=. Without one it uses the
invoice the job already names, else the invoice its accepted estimate
became, and refuses when there is neither. A button pressed from a status
it does not apply to is refused with the status named, so a finished job
cannot be put back on the road by a stale page.

The status is an ordinary field as well, so an owner can correct it on
the form; the save rules above still apply.
