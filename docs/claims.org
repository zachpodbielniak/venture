#+title: Employee reimbursements
#+description: Mileage and expense claims from submit through payment

* A claim is not yet a book

The =claims= module requires =finance= and =ledger=, and suggests =capture=
and =payables=. It owns =expense_claim= and =expense_claim_line=. Employees
file a draft, submit it, finance approves it, then payment posts through the
posting service: expense plus cash, or expense plus AP.

A mileage line is rate times miles, an exact decimal quantity with at most
three fractional digits, rounded half to even. A receipt line names a
=document= (or a capture original). The document checksum is copied onto
=receipt-hash= and a second line with that hash is refused.

Receipt uniqueness applies on edits as well as insertion. A submitted claim's lines cannot be edited or moved into another claim, preserving the evidence behind its frozen total.

Closed fiscal periods refuse submit, approve and pay. Generic status changes
are refused; only =VentureClaimsService= writes the workflow.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl claim submit 1
venturectl claim approve 1
venturectl claim pay 1
#+end_src

The web workbench is =/claims=. Settlement =cash= credits Cash 1000;
=payable= credits Accounts payable 2000. Missing control accounts reuse the
organization chart or =accounting_control_map=, as payables does.

Migration =000280_people.sql= verifies the claims (and payroll) table
families, or the absent set of a never-enabled module.

=test-claims= covers mileage arithmetic, submit/approve/pay posting,
duplicate receipt hashes, closed periods, generic status refusal, the module
switch and =/claims=.
