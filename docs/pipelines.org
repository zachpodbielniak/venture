#+title: Sales pipelines

* Configurable processes

The =pipelines= module requires =crm=. Its =pipeline=, =pipeline_stage=,
=deal_stage_entry= and =loss_reason= records use the ordinary field tables,
so their forms, JSON, lists and CLI CRUD share the same metadata.

A pipeline belongs to an organization. Its kind is =sales=, =renewal=,
=partnership= or =custom=. Stages have an ascending position, a probability,
a kind (=open=, =won=, =lost=), comma-separated required deal fields and a
rotting threshold in days. Zero disables that threshold.

Deals carry a pipeline, stage, owner username, next step, next-action time,
loss reason and loss note. =probability_overridden= preserves an explicitly
chosen probability on moves. =committed= marks a deal for the forecast.

* Moving

=venture_deal_service_move_stage()= is the transition boundary. It checks
organization and pipeline membership, required fields and active loss
reasons, refuses a stale deal version, and writes the deal and history in
one transaction. Inputs are unchanged; the return value is the updated deal.
A move to the existing stage creates no duplicate history. Generic updates
to either stage representation, pipeline or closing timestamp are refused
with =VentureDealService= named in the error.

The legacy enum remains. Won and lost stage kinds map to their matching
legacy outcomes. Open stages map positions 0, 1, 2 and 3 to =lead=,
=qualified=, =proposal= and =negotiation=; later positions map to
=negotiation=. Closing stamps =closed_at= and reopening clears it.

First use creates an organization's default Sales pipeline with all six
legacy stages. New deals retain their initial legacy stage and probability
for compatibility with existing import callers; subsequent changes use the
service. The initial history entry is an observation at creation, not an
invention of earlier stage transitions.

* Upgrade and reports

The paired =000090_pipelines.sql= migration records a durable pending backfill.
After metadata reconciliation and organization seeding, startup creates a
default for every organization and attaches retained deals through the same
initialization code. The backfill and clearing its pending marker share one
transaction. With pipelines disabled the marker remains pending; enabling it
on a later startup completes the work without creating optional tables early.
A retained deal's initial stage timestamp means observed at upgrade.

Reports accept =organization_id=, =pipeline_id= and =owner=. Forecast rows
are separate per pipeline, owner and currency: =open_weighted= is current
open value times probability; =committed= is full open value when probability
is at least 90 or the committed flag is set; =closed_value= is won value
whose =closed_at= falls in the period. These are sales forecasts, not invoices,
recognized revenue or cash.

=stage_duration= measures each stage visit's occupancy clipped to the period,
including open visits up to now, and reports average and median days.
=funnel= counts visits entered in the period and visits whose next transition
advanced to a higher open position or won before the period end. Repeated
visits count separately. =loss_reasons= groups lost deal count and value by
reason and currency in the closing period. The legacy =pipeline= report
also accepts =pipeline_id=.

=overdue_deals= exposes a metric with that key: current open deals whose
latest entry is older than their stage's positive =rotting_days=. A dashboard
=metric= widget with =report_name=overdue_deals= and =field=overdue_deals=
shows the count without a scheduled sweep.

* Entry points

=POST /api/v1/deals/:id/move= takes ={ "stage_id": 2, "note": "Qualified" }=.
Add =?stage=1= to propose it; approval rechecks the saved deal version and
stage rules through the service. Generic staged changes cannot bypass it.
The assistant's =venture_deal_move= always proposes a confirmation.

=venturectl deal move ID STAGE [NOTE]= calls the same action. The =/deals=
board groups cards by stage and provides a stage selector and Move button.
Set any required fields or loss reason through the deal's ordinary edit form
before moving it.

Archived deals receive their pipeline and stage references during upgrade,
without a fabricated entry referencing a deleted record. Their deletion
stamp remains intact. The upgrade test reopens the fixture file and verifies
that running startup again creates no duplicate history.

New deals require an active pipeline and satisfy the initial stage's required fields; a lost initial stage requires an active loss reason. Closed initial stages receive a closing timestamp. Historical backfill preserves prior state.

With sequences enabled, a won-stage move also applies enrollment exits in the same transaction. A refused exit rolls back the deal transition and stage history; callers continue to use the stage service rather than writing the legacy stage field.

* Deal lines

A deal can carry the products and services it is expected to close on.
=deal_line= is an ordinary field-table record: =deal_id=, optional
=product_id=, =description=, whole-unit =quantity=, exact =unit_price=
(=VentureMoney=), =discount_bp= as integer basis points (0-10000, so 1250
is 12.5%) and =position=. Its form, JSON, list, CLI CRUD and assistant
schema derive from the table, and a deal's page lists its lines beneath it.

=VentureDealService= owns the arithmetic. A line's value is quantity times
unit price less the discount, rounded once half to even through
=VentureMoney=; overflow is refused. While a deal has at least one line its
=value= is the sum of those lines, recomputed in the same transaction as
every line save, delete, restore or purge. A generic write that changes
=value= on such a deal is refused with an error naming =VentureDealService=;
other deal fields stay editable. A deal without lines keeps a manual value,
and removing the last line leaves the last derived amount in place and hands
the field back to manual editing. Lines must share one currency: a line in
another currency is refused and not persisted. Lines cannot move between
deals, and a line's deal and product must be live records in the same
organization. Three units at $19.99 with 1000 basis points off make $53.97.

* Create quote from deal

=venture_deal_service_create_quote()= copies the deal's lines into a draft
quote in one transaction: number =DEAL-<id>=, the deal's company, contact
and venture, the lines' currency, and one =quote_line= per deal line with
=discount_percent= equal to =discount_bp / 100= and no tax. The quote's
=deal_id= links it back, so the deal page lists its quotes and acceptance
moves the deal to won as [[file:quotes.org][Commercial quotes]] describes.
A deal line whose discount is not a whole percent (a multiple of 100 basis
points) cannot be copied and is refused before anything is written. A deal
with no lines is refused. Any failure after the quote header is written,
such as a line naming a since-deleted product, rolls the whole handoff back.

Running the action again never creates a second quote for the deal. It
revises the deal's latest quote through =VentureQuoteService= (=revise=),
which supersedes the previous revision and numbers the new one
=DEAL-<id>-R<n>=, then replaces the cloned lines with the deal's current
lines. An accepted quote is not revised; the refusal names the service.
The action requires the =quotes= module.

=POST /api/v1/deals/:id/quote= returns the new draft quote with status 201.
The deal page's *Create quote from lines* button posts to =/deals/:id/quote=
and redirects to the quote. =venturectl deal quote ID= calls the same route.

=test-deal-lines= covers the record, derived and manual values, the refused
generic value write, currency mismatch, invalid lines, the quote handoff,
its rerun as a revision, refusals with rollback, and the REST, page and
CLI surfaces.

* Sales targets and historical credit

The optional [[file:sales-performance.org][sales performance module]] places currency-separated
representative/team quotas beside the current forecast. It captures won
transitions and dated reversals atomically. Reassigning a won opportunity
does not rewrite its original representative's credit; value corrections
require reopening it first. Booked sales remain distinct from ledger revenue
and cash.
