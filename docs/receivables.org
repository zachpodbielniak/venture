* Receivables

The =receivables= module requires =finance= and =invoicing=. Its records are
field tables: the same declarations produce the database tables, forms,
REST resources, CLI commands, AI schemas and audit entries. Switching the
module off hides payments, credits, refunds and both reports together. Invoice events belong to invoicing, whose ledger dependency preserves issuance and voiding when receivables is disabled. Payment operations remain unavailable while receivables is off.

| Record | Meaning |
|--------+---------|
| =payment= | A customer's receipt: date, exact amount, method, reference and optional external ID |
| =payment_allocation= | An amount applied from exactly one payment or credit to an invoice |
| =customer_credit= | A credit note, deposit or overpayment, with a derived remaining balance |
| =refund= | Cash returned against an allocation or unused credit |
| =invoice_event= | The frozen issue amount and due date, or a subsequent workflow event |

A nonempty payment external ID is unique within its organization. The
service derives an immutable =external_key= from the organization and ID;
that field has a database UNIQUE constraint. Callers supply =external_id=,
not the derived key. An empty external ID is optional and may recur.

Posting uses mapped cash, receivables, income and tax control accounts; see [[file:setup.org][Accounting setup]].

* Receiving and allocating money

=VentureSettlementService= owns every financial invoice transition. A
generic write to =payment= reaches it through =venture_database_save()=,
so a form, REST request, =venturectl create payment= and an approved AI
write all perform the same transaction. The invoice page's Pay action
records a manual receipt for the remaining balance through that service.
Setting =status=paid= directly is refused with a validation error naming
=VentureSettlementService=.

For example, against an issued invoice for customer 1:

#+begin_src sh
venturectl create payment customer_id=1 invoice_id=1 \
  'amount=40 USD' date=2026-07-10 method=transfer external_id=bank-42
#+end_src

Without =invoice_id= the receipt remains a deposit. Allocate it later by
creating =payment_allocation= with =payment_id= or =credit_id=, an invoice,
date and amount. A receipt may settle several invoices. C callers can pass
an array of new allocations to =venture_settlement_service_apply_payment()=
to make the receipt and all allocations one transaction. An amount beyond
the invoice balance remains unused credit.

Enter a standalone credit as =customer_credit= with =kind=credit_note=.
Deposits and overpayments are created by receipts. The service derives
=remaining=; changing the stored balance is refused. A credit note applied
to an invoice reduces its balance without inventing a cash sale.

A =refund= names exactly one =allocation_id= or =credit_id=. Refunding an
applied receipt reopens the invoice by that amount and updates its cash
sale's refunded amount. Refunding unused credit reduces the credit. An
applied credit note is not a cash receipt and cannot be refunded through
the allocation path.

Every operation encloses its records, audit entries, cash sales, invoice
state and ledger batches in one database transaction. Any refusal rolls
the transaction back and restores caller-owned receipt/allocation objects.
Amounts must be positive and fit both the source and destination balances.
Customers and organizations must match. Issued invoice fields and lines,
receipts, allocations, credits, refunds and events cannot be rewritten or
deleted to change history. Dates cannot precede their source; invoice
events must be entered in chronological order, so a later refund cannot
fund an allocation in an earlier statement.

* Lifecycle and extensions

Create a draft, add lines, then send it. Sending freezes its amount, due date, and per-line income, discount, shipping
and tax allocations in an issue event. The state is =sent= before allocation,
=partially_paid= after a partial allocation, and =paid= at zero balance.
A refund derives the state again. Void is allowed for an unpaid invoice;
refund its applied cash first. A direct transition cannot undo a paid
state while retaining the allocation.

=VentureInvoiceStateMachine= declares the built-in edges in one table.
Its =transition= signal uses =G_SIGNAL_RUN_LAST=: ordinary handlers run
before the class handler, and the first FALSE vetoes the whole operation.
Keep external effects until after commit. Plugins can add named states
with a draft or sent financial phase and declare their edges without
editing the built-in table. Financial paid states stay derived.

* Historical balances

The =receivables= aging report reads issue events before the exclusive
period endpoint, subtracts dated allocations and adds dated refunds. An
invoice paid in August therefore remains in July's aging. An invoice first
issued in August does not enter July's figures. Aging uses the due date
frozen at issue and shows a separate bucket when none was supplied.

=customer_statement= requires =customer_id= and accepts =organization_id=
and =currency=. It shows the opening balance, dated movements and closing
balance. Invoice issues increase what the customer owes; payments and
credit notes reduce it; refunds increase it. Allocations move credit to
invoices without changing the customer's net balance. The service's
=venture_settlement_service_customer_balance()= reads the same events.

Reports read retained history, including soft-deleted rows. They select
one legal entity and currency and never convert or combine currencies.
Historical reconstruction requires issue events recorded by this module;
invoices predating its installation need an explicit migration of their
issue and payment history. This module does not invent that history from
the current status.

* Posting boundary

=venture_receivables_post_batch()= supplies balanced source-document legs to =venture_posting_service_post_entries()=. Journals are the sole accounting authority; generated cash-report sales bypass automatic sale posting to avoid recognizing the same receipt twice. Invoice document rules are refused because issuance and settlement already post their own journals.

Issuing debits receivables for the tax-inclusive total, credits income for
the frozen net (after discount, plus shipping) and credits sales tax payable
for the frozen tax. A receipt debits cash and
credits receivables. Unused receipts are credit balances in that control
account; allocation and creation of the receipt's credit post balanced
transfers within it. A credit note debits income and credits receivables;
a refund debits receivables and credits cash. Voiding reverses issuance.

The service properties =cash-account-id=, =receivable-account-id= and
=income-account-id= select accounts. Zero resolves active accounts 1000,
1100 and 4000 within the transaction's organization. Another organization
must have suitable accounts configured; the service does not create them.
Posting validates each batch's currency through the journal service. Different currencies are kept separate and are never converted. Every journal checks the fiscal calendar at its own effective date, so a February payment can settle a January invoice after January closes without rewriting January.

Existing cash reports still read sales: each cash allocation creates an immutable sale at the allocation date with external ID =allocation:<allocation UUID>=. Two partial payments therefore remain distinct even when their invoice number is the same. An allocation refund creates a negative sale at the refund date with external ID =refund:<refund UUID>= and records its ID in =refund.sale_id=. Neither operation rewrites the original receipt. Generic edits, deletion, purge and explicit document reposting of these projections are refused.

* Integrated verification

The accounting-cycle regression issues a $100 invoice, receives $40, closes January, receives the remaining $60 in February and refunds $10 later in February. It checks actual cash, receivables and income account balances after every step, preserves January's balance and refuses editing the original cash projection. Separate HTTP coverage verifies customer-statement options reach the report and module-off coverage verifies invoice issuance still works.

Issued invoice print and detail tables show frozen Net and Tax per line next to
the line total, so tax is never presented as revenue.

* Tax exemption

A company or invoice may be marked =tax-exempt= with an optional reason
(for example a non-profit certificate). At issuance the settlement service
copies the customer's exemption onto the invoice, freezes tax at zero even
when lines still carry a tax percent, and posts AR equal to net income.
Print and invoice detail show the exemption. Tax-allocation corrections skip
voided and exempt invoices.
