#+title: Accounting backup and restore
#+description: Export an organization pack and restore into an empty org

=VentureBackupService= exports the chart (code, name, kind), posted journals,
invoices, bills and bank accounts as JSON or a CSV summary. Restore recreates
accounts with their original kinds, posts journals, and issues invoices that
were sent. Receipts, tax freezes and allocations are not yet in the pack.
Restore requires an organization with no journals. A second restore into the
same books is refused.
