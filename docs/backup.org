#+title: Accounting backup and restore
#+description: Export an organization pack and restore into an empty org

=VentureBackupService= exports a versioned JSON pack: chart (code, name,
kind), non-settlement posted journals, invoices with frozen line tax,
invoice events, receipts, allocations, bills and bank accounts. Restore
recreates accounts, posts opening journals, issues sent invoices (which
rebuilds tax freeze and invoice journals), then applies receipts through
=VentureSettlementService= so allocations and cash match the source.
Settlement journals are not replayed as raw rows. Restore requires an
organization with no journals. A second restore into the same books is
refused.
