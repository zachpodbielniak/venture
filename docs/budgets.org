#+title: Budgets and cash forecasting
#+description: Planned amounts, vs-actual and unpaid AR/AP cash forecast

* Budgets

The =budgets= module requires =statements=. It owns =budget= and =budget_line=.
A plan names a period (=YYYY= or =YYYY-MM=), a book currency and an optional
default dimension. Each line names an account, a period, an amount and an
optional dimension.

Period strings must match =YYYY= or =YYYY-MM= exactly; trailing text and out-of-range dates are refused. Named report periods use their calendar bounds rather than their display label.

=VentureBudgetService= compares those lines with posted ledger movement from
=VentureLedgerBalances=. Expense actuals are period debits less credits;
income actuals are credits less debits. Variance is actual less budget.

* Cash forecast

The cash forecast starts from unpaid receivables and payables (control-account
closing balances) and adds remaining budget inflows less remaining budget
outflows for the same period.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl budget vs-actual 2026-08
venturectl budget forecast 2026-08
#+end_src

The web page is =/budgets=. Turning the module off hides the records, both
reports and the page.
