#+title: Budgets and cash forecasting
#+description: Planned amounts, vs-actual and unpaid AR/AP cash forecast

* Budgets

The =budgets= module requires =statements=. It owns =budget= and =budget_line=.
A plan names a period (=YYYY= or =YYYY-MM=), a book currency and an optional
default dimension. Each line names an account, a period, an amount and an
optional dimension.

Period strings must match =YYYY= or =YYYY-MM= exactly; trailing text and out-of-range dates are refused. Named report periods use their calendar bounds rather than their display label.

=VentureBudgetService= compares those lines with posted ledger movement from
=VentureLedgerBalances=. Expense actuals are period debits less credits;
income actuals are credits less debits. Variance is actual less budget.

* Cash forecast

The cash forecast starts from unpaid receivables and payables (control-account
closing balances) and adds remaining budget inflows less remaining budget
outflows for the same period.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl budget vs-actual 2026-08
venturectl budget forecast 2026-08
#+end_src

The web page is =/budgets=. Turning the module off hides the records, both
reports and the page.

* Monthly category envelopes

An envelope is an ordinary =budget_line= with =envelope= enabled. Create an
active budget, then a line for each category's expense account. Set the line's
=period= to its first month (=YYYY-MM=), its =amount= to the monthly allocation
in the organization's book currency, and choose =rollover= independently for
each line. Leave Dimension empty. Each organization can have only one live
envelope per expense account, including draft plans; edit or remove the old
line before replacing it. Future start months and inactive or deleted plans
are not shown. Ordinary business lines keep their existing period rules.

The account is the category boundary. The expense posting profile already
maps category codes to expense accounts; choose those same accounts here.
Categories mapped to the same account share one envelope. Give categories
separate expense accounts and posting mappings when they need separate limits.
Bank transactions must be categorized and posted before they count as spending.
Own-account transfers and card principal repayments do not hit expense accounts;
fees and interest posted to an expense account do count. Refunds reduce spending.

The monthly amount repeats from the start month, including months when no
report was opened. With rollover off, Available is that month's amount. With
rollover on, Rolled over is all earlier monthly allocations minus posted net
spending since the start month. Both unused money and overspending carry over,
including across year boundaries. Available is Monthly amount plus Rolled over;
Left to spend is Available minus this month's Spent. These are planning amounts,
not claims about the cash balance of a bank account, and no journal is created.

Status is On track until at least 80% of Available is spent, then Running low.
Exactly zero left is Used up; negative left is Over budget. Warnings are shown
in the report when it is opened, not delivered as notifications. Calculations
use integer minor units and refuse overflow and foreign-currency allocations.
Editing an envelope's amount, start month or rollover choice recomputes its
history from the current plan and posted ledger; it does not freeze past months.

The household Budgets page defaults to Monthly envelopes and the current month.
Select a month to see its amounts, statuses and total left to spend. The page
links to the generated Budget and Budget line forms. Business viewers can select
Monthly envelopes explicitly, while vs actual and cash forecast remain available
for ordinary budget lines. Envelopes are separate from those two business reports.
The API accepts an explicit =organization_id= and the UI follows the selected
organization, within the normal access rules.

#+begin_src sh
venturectl budget envelopes 2027-01
#+end_src

The shared report is =budget_envelopes=; the direct endpoint is
=/api/v1/budget_reports?kind=envelopes&period=2027-01=. Migration
=000982_monthly_envelopes.sql= preserves existing business lines with both new
flags off, on SQLite and PostgreSQL. An absent budgets table is an empty upgrade
case; fresh rows get their defaults from the field metadata.
