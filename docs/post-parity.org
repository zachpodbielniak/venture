* Delivery contract

Issue 125 is delivered on =epic/post-parity-5= as one pull request. Its
starting point is master =848a93d=. Independent implementation lanes use
worktrees; at most three agents run concurrently. Integrated increments
must pass a DEBUG build and suite before the next increment is accepted.
The pull request remains WIP until its requirements and review fixes have
been verified. Passing unit tests alone establishes neither replacement
readiness nor hosted-launch readiness.

The operator authorized choosing defaults on 2026-09-19 and explicitly
excluded Lightsite provisioning. No service creation, entitlement changes,
suspension or cancellation is sent to Lightsite. Its existing ownership of
campaign pages is unchanged. The separate form/capture integration contract
remains in scope. Previously deferred payroll tax calculation, mobile,
paid enrichment, voice/live chat and broad Money navigation refactoring
remain deferred.

* Selected defaults

** Projects and billing

Reuse client projects, tickets/worklogs, approved project time/cost,
quotes, progress invoicing and settlement. Support time and materials and
fixed contractual milestones, with partial progress invoicing and retainers
through the existing services. Project scope, ownership, delivery acceptance
and changes are explicit records/actions. Staff authorize scope and
acceptance; customer portals retain their existing document visibility and
quote acceptance boundary. New customer task editing is excluded.

Project financials distinguish contractual budget, billed allocations,
approved unbilled work, recorded actual cost and management profit. Freeze
labour cost at time approval using the applicable rate; unknown historical
cost remains unknown rather than being recomputed from today's rate.
Separate currencies and retain source references. Management profit is not
a substitute for posted general-ledger revenue recognition. Reporting must
state its date basis and treatment of corrections.

** Tenant boundary

A tenant is an isolated workspace containing one or more legal/accounting
organizations. A customer company is never a tenant. The hosted default is
one application process and PostgreSQL database/role per tenant, plus
separate state, attachment and backup roots. Platform routing/provisioning
selects the tenant; organization selectors never select another database.
Trusted platform operators own executable plugins and host configuration.
Tenant administration is distinct from platform administration.

This topology preserves the current main-context database ownership and
process-wide registries. Do not introduce shared-table hosting by assuming
that the organization picker is a tenant boundary. Any future shared-table
implementation requires its own database-enforced isolation contract and
adversarial qualification.

** Integrations and subscriptions

Organization integrations are explicitly configured, disabled or assigned
an eligible platform service. Missing configuration fails closed. Stripe
has one account per organization; historical account and test/live binding
survive credential rotation and account replacement. A personal mailbox or
calendar remains private to its authorized user.

Subscription collection uses verified provider settlement, durable attempt
identity and explicit failures/recovery. Invoice generation is not payment.
No Lightsite provisioning is attempted. Platform AI grants must attribute
usage to the organization, enforce quotas and fail closed on revocation;
private provider failure must never silently fall back to shared access.

** Operational qualification

Use synthetic data. Proposed service qualification profiles are 100, 1,000
and 3,000 provisioned tenants, each with 10 users, 100,000 business rows and
1 GiB of attachments. At least 10 percent of tenants are active at once,
with two concurrent users per active tenant and one ordinary request per
user per second. Report the actual hardware, workload mix, active process
count, event/job rates and measured storage footprint with every result.
Targets are p95 below 500 ms for ordinary reads, below 1 second for ordinary
writes, and fewer than 0.1 percent unexpected errors; bounded background
jobs are measured separately. These are acceptance targets, not measured
capacity claims.

Default recovery targets are a 15-minute recovery point and a four-hour
single-tenant restore time. Require encrypted off-site backups and a restore
drill that reconciles financial and operational references while a neighbor
continues running. Accounting export alone is not a tenant backup. Retain
daily operational backups for 30 days by default; statutory accounting
retention and legal holds must be configurable and are not erased by that
expiry. No jurisdictional or certification claim is made. Hosted launch
requires an independent isolation/security review and recorded deployment
evidence.
