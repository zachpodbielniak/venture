#+title: SaaS billing
#+description: Plans, subscription terms and recurring customer revenue

* The billing module

=billing= requires =invoicing= and =receivables=. Plans and prices describe
what Lightsite sells: Starter and Pro, each with monthly and yearly prices.
=customer_subscription= is the customer's commitment. The example plugin's
=subscription= remains a recurring cost paid by the business.

=plan= carries an organization, optional venture, name, code, description
and active flag. Codes are unique within the organization. =plan_price=
separates currency, interval, exact amount, seat pricing and trial length
from the plan. =subscription_event= preserves changes to those commitments.
=dunning_step= defines notice, retry, pause and cancel actions by overdue
day; =billing_notice= records notices with an email channel.

Revenue recognition schedules are a follow-up. A subscription's contracted
recurring revenue, an issued invoice and a cash receipt are different facts.
There is no payment provider, card storage or mail transport in this module.
