#+title: SaaS billing
#+description: Plans, subscription terms and recurring customer revenue

* Selling Lightsite

=billing= requires =invoicing= and =receivables=. A =plan= describes Starter
or Pro; a =plan_price= describes one version of its monthly or yearly terms.
The customer's commitment is a =customer_subscription=. The example
plugin's =subscription= remains a recurring cost paid by the business.

The field tables supply ordinary lists, forms, REST resources, CLI records,
assistant schemas and audit history. Plans and prices are edited through
those generated surfaces. Subscription state is changed through
=VentureBillingService=, including when an instruction comes from a form,
the CLI, REST, automation or an approved assistant proposal.

| Record | Meaning |
|--------+---------|
| =plan= | Organization, optional venture, name, organization-unique code, description, active flag |
| =plan_price= | Plan, currency, month/year interval, exact amount, per-seat flag, trial days, active flag |
| =customer_subscription= | Customer/contact, price, state, seats, period, trial and cancellation dates, external identity; pending terms and adjustment |
| =subscription_event= | Immutable effective-dated change, old/new terms and seats, proration, invoice and frozen MRR |
| =dunning_step= | Active notice, retry, pause or cancel action at an overdue-day offset |
| =billing_notice= | Durable email-channel notice; no email is sent |
| =billing_request= | Stageable instruction and, after execution, its immutable result |

Plan codes and nonempty subscription external IDs are unique within their
organization, including retained history. Empty external IDs are optional.
A price used by a subscription or event is immutable except for its active
flag: make a new price version rather than changing an existing customer's
terms underneath them. Customer, contact and price must belong to the same
organization, and the contact must belong to the customer company.

For example, with organization 1, Lightsite venture 1 and customer company 1
already present (use the IDs returned by each create):

#+begin_src sh
venturectl create plan organization_id=1 venture_id=1 name=Starter code=starter active=true
venturectl create plan organization_id=1 venture_id=1 name=Pro code=pro active=true
venturectl create plan_price organization_id=1 plan_id=1 currency=USD interval=month amount='30 USD' per_seat=true trial_days=14 active=true
venturectl create plan_price organization_id=1 plan_id=1 currency=USD interval=year amount='300 USD' per_seat=true active=true
venturectl create plan_price organization_id=1 plan_id=2 currency=USD interval=month amount='60 USD' per_seat=true active=true
venturectl create plan_price organization_id=1 plan_id=2 currency=USD interval=year amount='600 USD' per_seat=true active=true
venturectl billing start organization_id=1 company_id=1 plan_price_id=1 seats=2 external_id=lightsite-customer-1 --as-of 2026-01-01
venturectl billing renew --as-of 2026-01-15 --dry-run
venturectl billing renew --as-of 2026-01-15
venturectl billing change 1 plan_price=3 at_period_end=true --as-of 2026-01-20
#+end_src

A non-trial start issues its first period's invoice immediately. A trial
start issues nothing until its trial ends; renewal then activates it and
issues its first paid period. Seats default to one and must be positive.
Non-seat prices ignore seat count when calculating charges.

* Actions and transactions

=billing start= creates a subscription. The other single-record actions take
its ID: =renew=, =change=, =change-seats=, =pause=, =resume=, =cancel=,
=mark-payment-failed= and =recover=. =change= accepts =plan_price= as a CLI
alias for =plan_price_id=. =change= and =cancel= accept
=at_period_end=true=. Seat changes are immediate.

A scheduled plan change keeps today's price until renewal. Scheduled
cancellation keeps the subscription active until its boundary, when a
renewal sweep cancels it without another period invoice. Immediate
cancellation ends the commitment at the action date. Events are
chronological; a request pinned to an older subscription version is refused.

Each instruction holds one transaction across subscriptions, events,
invoices, invoice lines, settlement, journals, notices and audit entries.
An injected failure after an invoice draft is saved rolls everything back.
The service works on detached request data and only copies results back
after success. Generic subscription, event, notice and completed-request
writes, deletion, restoration and purge are refused naming the service.

The =changing= signal runs after the tentative subscription write and
before event persistence, inside the transaction. It receives a detached
event snapshot. Handlers run before the class closure and may return an
owned =GError= to veto; the whole transaction rolls back. Handlers must not
send mail or perform another external effect before commit.

Invoices use the subscription's company and contact, and the plan's venture.
Their lines carry exactly the price currency; there is no conversion. A
stable subscription/period invoice number also prevents a period being
invoiced twice. Every invoice is issued through =VentureSettlementService=.
The fiscal-period guard checks its issue date. A late sweep dates invoices
at the supplied effective date while retaining the service period in the
stable invoice identity. The organization needs the settlement accounts
configured in [[file:receivables.org][receivables]].

* Proration

Periods are half-open. Changes allocate the full-period price difference
with =VentureMoney= over its calendar days, then sum only the remaining
days. Remainder cents stay on their originally assigned days. Monthly
boundaries retain their original day: January 31, February 28, March 31.

Worked example: two Starter seats at $30 per seat were billed $60 on
January 1 for January 1 through February 1. A third seat is added January 17.
The full-period difference is $30, or 3,000 cents. Allocating over 31 days
gives 97 cents to the first 24 days and 96 cents to the last seven. The
remaining fifteen days receive =8 * 97 + 7 * 96 = 1,448= cents: *$14.48*.
The subscription's MRR becomes $90 immediately. Its next invoice charges
$90 plus the $14.48 pending adjustment: *$104.48*.

A decrease of one seat on the same day produces *-$14.48*. Renewal issues
its ordinary $30 invoice, creates a $14.48 customer credit through
settlement, and applies it, leaving *$15.52* outstanding. Credit beyond an
invoice's amount remains customer credit. Adjustments accumulate exactly
until renewal. Trials and scheduled changes add no immediate adjustment.

Price versions may use different minor-unit exponents in the same currency; credit allocation compares their monetary values with checked rescaling.
Changing currency is refused. A change between monthly and yearly terms
normalizes both prices to the service period already in progress before
allocation, including repeated interval changes within that period.

Cancellation does not automatically refund an already invoiced period.
Pending adjustments remain explicit on a subscription cancelled before
renewal and require a deliberate final invoice or credit decision. There
is no implicit refund or payment-provider request.

* Renewal and dunning sweeps

#+begin_src sh
venturectl billing renew --as-of 2026-04-01 --dry-run
venturectl billing renew --as-of 2026-04-01
venturectl billing dunning --as-of 2026-04-05
#+end_src

Omitting the date uses the server's clock. Sweeps select one organization
(the configured default unless =organization_id= is supplied). Renewal
catches up every due period, capped at 1,200 periods per subscription in
one instruction; repeating a completed sweep creates no invoices. Dry run
returns the number of operations without writing records or audit entries.
Schedule the CLI or POST the sweep endpoint from the installation's
scheduler; no separate billing thread touches the database.

=mark-payment-failed= records the failure date and moves active to
=past_due=. Dunning measures whole overdue days from that date. Each
subscription/failure episode/step has one durable =billing_notice= with
=channel=email=. Notice and retry actions record that intent only; pause
and cancel also call the subscription lifecycle service. Steps run by day
offset, then ID; escalation continues after a dunning pause until cancellation.
A late sweep catches up all eligible steps in that order. =resume= clears
the failure episode. =recover= moves
=past_due= back to active. The operator or a future collection adapter
reports failure and recovery; an unpaid invoice alone does not fabricate a
failed provider attempt.

The mail lane must wire the =billing_notice= adapter to =VentureMailer=.
This module imports no mail header and sends no mail. It integrates no
payment provider and stores no card data.

* Surfaces and approval

The Money sidebar links to the subscriptions list. Subscription pages have
renew, pause, resume, cancel and change actions. REST uses
=POST /api/v1/customer_subscriptions/:id/renew|change|cancel|pause|resume=;
additional service verbs use the same shape. Start and sweeps use
=POST /api/v1/billing/start|renew-sweep|dunning-sweep=. Bodies use underscores.

Every action accepts =?stage=1=; CLI actions accept =--stage=. The assistant
can stage a =billing_request= through its ordinary generated create tool.
The request's fields show the proposed action rather than granting a generic
writer permission to set a derived subscription status. Staging pins the
subscription version; approval rechecks version, state, currency and fiscal
period. Approval after another action is refused as a conflict.

* Report definitions

All three reports live in the report registry and accept =organization_id=.
=mrr= and =churn= select one =currency= (the configured default unless
supplied); they never add currencies. Their notes carry these definitions:

- =mrr=: contracted active and past-due MRR immediately before the exclusive
  period endpoint, per plan and in total. Trials, pauses, cancellation and
  expiry contribute zero. Annual prices divide by twelve with half-even
  rounding; ARR is twelve times rounded MRR. Frozen event amounts preserve
  the answer after later changes. =as_of= can narrow the endpoint.
- Movements within the period: =new= is first positive MRR, =reactivation=
  a subsequent return from zero, =expansion= and =contraction= changes
  between positive amounts, =churn= a fall to zero, including pause.
  All movement metrics are positive magnitudes.
- =churn=: opening companies with positive MRR are the cohort. A logo is
  lost when its combined closing subscriptions have zero MRR. Revenue loss
  sums positive opening-minus-closing differences per opening company,
  including contraction, excluding new companies and gains. Rates are
  also returned in basis points (10,000 = 100%); an empty denominator has
  no rate metric. Numerators and denominators remain visible.
- =subscriptions_due=: the current operational renewal queue from the
  period endpoint through the next =days=, default 30, inclusive. It
  excludes scheduled cancellations and paused/past-due subscriptions.
  It is a forward-looking queue, not reconstructed historical schedules.

These are contracted revenue metrics, not cash receipts or accounting
revenue recognition. Usage metering, entitlements, payment integration and
revenue recognition schedules remain separate follow-ups.

* Upgrade and verification

Migration =000170_billing_schema.sql= is paired for SQLite and PostgreSQL.
After metadata adds tables and indexes, it verifies organization-scoped
plan/subscription identities when those tables exist. Disabled billing is
supported. Existing operational data remains untouched; there is no
inference of historical subscription or financial events.

=test-billing= covers the records, first invoice, trials, state transitions,
proration and credits, renewal retries and backlog, dry runs, dunning,
rollback, price history, customer scope, period refusal, currency refusal,
annual normalization, scheduled terms, migrations and report definitions.
=test-billing-surfaces= exercises real REST, web actions, CLI subprocesses,
staged approval and stale assistant proposals. The shared module, report,
migration and authentication suites cover their integration points.
